# Template — Model, complete, reduce cues, explain independently

- Pattern: worked-example-fading, version 1
- Basis: [Atkinson/Renkl/Merrill and Renkl/Atkinson/Große source records](../../docs/instructional-pattern-sources.md)
- Status: reusable draft template and original draft exemplar
- Fill the [common contract](README.md#common-authoring-contract).

## Copy and fill

State one invariant goal and a controlled task family before designing the ladder:

| Stage | Teacher/solution support | Learner response structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full model | Complete worked representation and why each step is valid | Study, reconstruct and explain the exemplar |
| Completion | Some steps supplied; a later step omitted | Fill missing parts and explain their meaning |
| Sparse cues | Relationship/goal supplied, worked steps removed | Choose and label the steps; construct a reason |
| Independent | Fresh task without solution cues | Choose a representation and explain from blank space |
| Changed application | Same taught relationship in a new form | Detect/justify/transfer; inspect reasoning, not surface copying |

Write a full key and criterion for each stage. Choose what fades explicitly:
worked steps, strategy hints, sentence stems, diagram labels or an exemplar answer.
Changing response structure does not change the required mathematical content.
Keep the underlying relationship and numerical demand stable while testing the
effect of fewer cues. Introduce a harder range separately.

Observe a correct fresh attempt and reason before removing a needed cue; restore
the specific support if performance breaks down. Do not force a fixed fade rate
or withdraw read-aloud, counters, drawing, pointing or a scribe as a reward.
An independent response means no solution cue, under the declared access supports.

## Original Grade 2 ladder

C-MATH-001 / U-MATH-001-01 / L-MATH-001-01-01; O-MATH-001-001, unpacking one
ten while preserving the amount; 0–99. Entry: a ten contains ten one-units.
Material: counters/bundles/chart and paper. Allow about 20–25 minutes initially,
split or repeat as needed; advancement depends on evidence, not elapsed time.

| Stage/task | Learner-facing content | Full teacher key/criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F0, full model | “34 is 3 tens + 4 ones. Unpack one ten: 2 tens + 14 ones. The amount stays 34 because one ten equals ten ones.” Rebuild and explain why. | 30 + 4 = 20 + 14; equal-valued groups were exchanged |
| F1, completion | “45 = 4 tens + 5 ones = 3 tens + __ ones. It stays 45 because __.” | 15; one ten became ten ones and no amount was lost/added |
| F2, sparse response | Unpack one ten from 56. Before: __ tens + __ ones. After: __ tens + __ ones. Give your own reason. | 5 tens + 6 ones = 4 tens + 16 ones; same fifty-six one-units |
| F3, blank response | Show 67 before and after unpacking one ten. Explain why the amount is unchanged. | 6 tens + 7 ones = 5 tens + 17 ones; choose own accurate model and reason |
| F4, changed application | A learner unpacks one ten from 78 and writes “6 tens + 8 ones.” Test and repair the claim. | Correct: 6 tens + 18 ones = 78; proposed 68 loses ten |

F1 supplies a sentence frame; F2 keeps unit labels but removes its reason frame;
F3 keeps the mathematical demand with no response template. The goal is increasing
ownership of the model and reason, not fewer words or less rigorous evidence.
F4 is a modest error-analysis application, not a far-transfer guarantee.

Grammar names the units, logic explains the invariant, and rhetoric communicates
it in the learner's chosen accessible form. Each row is a teaching exemplar;
do not call a repeated F3 response independent after its solution was shown.
These tasks cover only the tens/ones component, not the entire course outcome.

## Authoring checks

Check all stages for equivalent unit values and consistent “unpack one ten.”
Verify that blanks genuinely remove support, full keys exist, and an open
response can still satisfy the same result/model/reason criterion. If a learner
can fill blanks but cannot model the exchange, restore the concrete relationship
rather than count blank completion as understanding.
