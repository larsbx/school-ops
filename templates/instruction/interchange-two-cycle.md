# Template — Two-cycle mathematical communication

- Pattern: interchange-two-cycle, version 1
- Structural source: [Cambridge reference](../../docs/interchange-source-notes.md)
- Status: reusable draft template; completed exemplar below is also draft
- Fill course/unit/lesson, audience, range, outcomes, prerequisites, materials,
  support, budget and evidence using the [common contract](README.md#common-authoring-contract).

## Copy and fill

1. **Cycle A goal:** [relationship to investigate and what a recipient must reconstruct].
2. **Context/model:** [concrete question; short original exchange with a mathematical reason].
3. **Focus/practice:** [names/notation, model, guided and independent tasks with keys].
4. **Partner exchange:** [different information for each participant; observable shared result].
5. **Cycle B goal:** [related inverse, extension or application, with its own practice/exchange].
6. **Independent application:** [fresh task, permitted support and criterion].
7. **Workbook/teacher links:** [separate learner work, complete keys and next-step decisions].

Cycle does not mean one lesson or a fixed duration. Rearrange exercises for
prerequisites and purpose; do not copy language-course dialogue into mathematics.
A teacher can be the partner, and a drawing can communicate a reason.

## Original Grade 2 exemplar

Scope: C-MATH-001 / U-MATH-001-01 / L-MATH-001-01-01; tens/ones component of
O-MATH-001-001; 0–99. Entry: count single objects and identify the one-unit.
Materials: counters/ten-bundles, paper and separate partner cards.

| Stage | Original task | Teacher key/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cycle A model | Group 14 counters; describe what the group contains | 1 ten + 4 ones; all fourteen counted units remain |
| Partner A clue | Describe 2 tens and 4 ones without showing the card | Recipient reconstructs 24 |
| Partner B clue | Describe 5 tens and no extra ones | Recipient reconstructs 50 and explains zero extra ones |
| Cycle B model | Unpack one ten from 21 | 2 tens + 1 one = 1 ten + 11 ones |
| Exchange task | Describe 1 ten and 16 ones; recipient packs complete tens | 2 tens + 6 ones = 26; amount conserved |
| Independent application | Show 46 before and after unpacking one ten; explain | 4 tens + 6 ones = 3 tens + 16 ones; same forty-six one-units |

Both partners' agreement needs a checked model; talk alone is not correctness.
Require names (grammar), conservation (logic) and a recipient-readable explanation
(rhetoric), with objects/drawings allowed.

The complete [M01 pilot](../../curriculum/mathematics/grade-2/units/place-value/two-cycle/README.md)
supplies learner pages, workbook, separate cards, teacher scripts/keys and formative
review. Its two 25-minute sessions replace the standard 25-minute lesson
implementation; additional workbook/review budgets are explicit there.
This exemplar checks no hundreds and does not establish the full course outcome.

## Choose, adapt or combine

Use for communicating representations and reasoning. For a small procedural gap,
a brief [practice/feedback loop](khan-practice-feedback.md) may fit better.
If explanation prompts supply too much of the answer, apply
[fading](worked-example-fading.md) inside a cycle while retaining the criterion.
