# Composition, difference, and parity — unit check

A-MATH-001-05 · Draft · Untimed

Use counters or drawings. Explain your thinking in words or by showing your model.

1. Split 17 counters into a part of 9 and a second part. Find the second part and use addition to check.
2. There are 15 paper clips on a mat. Move 6 off the mat. How many remain? Model the change and name the unit.
3. One box holds 14 crayons and another holds 8. How many more does the first hold? Show how you know without moving crayons out of either box.
4. Pair all 19 counters as far as possible. Is 19 even or odd? Explain what happens to the leftover if one counter is added.
5. A basket contains 8 apples. Put 7 more in it. How many are in it now? Show what you joined and name the unit.
