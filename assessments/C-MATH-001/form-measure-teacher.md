## Reproducible arithmetic checks

1. Unit squares: 3×3 = 9, 1×9 = 9, 2×2 = 4, 1×4 = 4; 4×4 = 16, 4×2 = 8, 2×3 = 6, 2×4 = 8, 1×7 = 7.
2. Rods: 7×2 = 14; five rods × 2 = 10; 12 − 10 = 2; six rods × 2 = 12.
3. Line: 2 + 7 − 3 = 6; 11 − 3 = 8. Tick labels count is one more than unit intervals.
4. Every response assumes the given equal-size units and an unobstructed straight line; do not silently substitute a ruler with unknown calibration.
