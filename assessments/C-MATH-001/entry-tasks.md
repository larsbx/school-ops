# Entry conversation - learner sheet

A-MATH-001-01 • Grade 2 • draft • prerequisite sampling

Use counters, paper, pencil and an optional blank tens/ones chart.
Directions may be read aloud. You may speak, draw, point or use a scribe.
There is no speed requirement. Use working paper for models.

## E1

Count a set of 18 counters, then show a different arrangement with the same total.

## E2

Count aloud from 96 through 102.

## E3

Write the numeral for sixty-three; say how many tens and ones it has.

## E4

Which is greater, 54 or 45? Explain with objects or a drawing.

## E5

Find 8 + 7 and describe a method.

## E6

Find 14 - 6 and show what the subtraction means.

Show your work on working paper or to your teacher. This is a conversation about
starting skills, not a timed test or a pass mark for a whole course.
