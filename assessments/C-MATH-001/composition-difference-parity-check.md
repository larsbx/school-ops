# Composition, difference, and parity — teacher design and key

- ID: A-MATH-001-05
- Status: draft
- Scope: Grade 2, [U-MATH-001-02](../../curriculum/mathematics/grade-2/units/composition-difference-parity/README.md), quantities 0–20
- Purpose: collect model, result and reasoning evidence after the relevant lessons
- Allow about 15 minutes for planning; no deadline. Split sittings if needed.

Distribute only the [learner tasks](composition-difference-parity-tasks.md). Provide identical counters, paper and pencil. Permit read-aloud and oral, drawn, pointed or scribed responses. Keep keys out of view; avoid solution cues. If a prompt has been rehearsed with its answer, review a fresh equivalent before independent use.

| Task | Outcome | Complete key and criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | O-MATH-001-005 | 8 counters. Partition the 17 into disjoint parts of 9 and 8; show 17 − 9 = 8 and reconstruct 9 + 8 = 17, or show equivalent model relations. |
| 2 | O-MATH-001-006 | 9 paper clips. Model removal of 6 from 15; 15 − 6 = 9. Identify the unit and explain that 9 remain on the mat. |
| 3 | O-MATH-001-006 | 6 crayons. Match eight across the two rows; six are unmatched in the longer row. The difference 14 − 8 = 6 describes comparison, without changing either collection. |
| 4 | O-MATH-001-013 (pairing only) | Nine disjoint pairs and one leftover: odd. Adding one counter completes a tenth pair, making 20 even. Require the pairing model and reason. |
| 5 | O-MATH-001-006 | 15 apples. Join the original eight and seven added apples: 8 + 7 = 15. Explain why both groups belong to the final collection. |

Use outcome-specific evidence rather than a total score. For 006 inspect joining, removal and comparison separately. Record independent, supported, needs review or not assessed for each task. A result without the requested model or reason is incomplete; review and reassess with fresh tasks. Pairing does not certify all of 013.

Before readiness review, confirm taught scope, print/access conditions, local materials, appropriate pacing, and prompt/key suitability. Keep actual learner records private.
