**Administration proposed, not approved:** untimed with breaks; optional planning allowance 20–30 minutes (not a time limit). Blank paper and hundreds/tens/ones or tactile objects; equal-unit number-line marks, read-aloud, scribed/oral/signed/AAC/pointing responses without mathematical prompts. Provide each answer with its **counted unit** and a drawing, model or reason. The examples describe fictional objects/counts, not observed data or genuine purchases.

## U6-01 — Regroup and explain

A classroom has **76 blue counters** and **58 yellow counters**. How many **counters altogether**? Model an exchange of ones/tens into tens/hundreds, then check using another operation.

## U6-02 — Work back from a total

There are **143 spaces** in a drawing. **67 spaces** are marked. How many are not marked? Show how you know your answer recombines with 67 to make 143.

## U6-03 — Answer the story's question

A display contains **96 paper shapes**. Volunteers remove **48 paper shapes**. What quantity remains? Explain why this is a subtraction, not an addition or a question about equal-size groups.

## U6-04 — Respect an equal scale

On an evenly spaced marked number line, start at **70**. Move forward **25** units, then backward **13** units. Give the intermediate and final marks and a reason the movements match the marked distances.

## U6-05 — Records and limits

A *fictional* project table gives **Day A: 14 collected stamps**, **Day B: 9**, **Day C: 16**. Draw a three-row table. Find the recorded total and how many more Day C has than Day B. A peer predicts **Day D: 23**. Does the supplied table establish that count? Explain.

## U6-06 — Which plan is defensible?

You need **26 art cards**. Plan A supplies **three packs of 9 cards**. Plan B supplies **two packs of 14 cards**. Both options are available. Choose the plan producing the **fewest extra cards**; show both totals/extras and explain whether this also establishes the *cheapest* plan.
