### Arithmetic and category audit (original constructed reference)

- Five fictional dated rows: May **5, 6, 9, 13, 16** (all within one month; no leap/month-crossing issue).
- Explicit day-gap checks: `9 - 5 = 4`, `13 - 6 = 7`, `16 - 5 = 11`. These are calendar calculations, **not** astronomical cycle lengths.
- `seen = 3`, `clear/not-seen = 1`, `blocked/not-checked = 1` → five mutually exclusive record states.
- `clear-view = 4`, `view blocked = 1` → five complete rows.
- In all labels, “not seen” means **not seen from one specified view**; “not checked” means missing observation.

## Proposed outcome-specific decision (human review pending)

**O-MATH-001-010 — actual records + time and evidence limits:**

1. **S5-02 and S5-05:** both elapsed-day counts correct with units and explanation, including correct calendar-week interpretation; else revisit date intervals.
2. **S5-03 and S5-07:** both explicitly reject unjustified sky-absence/cycle claims and state the missing evidence; else reteach observation-vs-inference.
3. **S5-09: mandatory for full O010 claim:** independently verifiable, safe/accessible dated records of a sky feature on at least two distinct dates, accurate interval, justified comparison and limit, **after** owner approves source/safety/alternative route. If not provided, mark O010 **not yet assessed** even if S5-02/03/05/07 are all correct. No exposure of child location or personal identifiers.

**O-MATH-001-011 — table/diagram and prediction boundary:**

1. **S5-01 and S5-08:** accurate record versus unsupported prediction and differentiated non-observation claims.
2. **S5-04 and S5-06:** all labels, counts and uniform units accurate, plus explanation of what is **not** measured.
3. Any wrong or omitted component triggers feedback and a **fresh, unseen** check; no weighted average compensates.

**No scalar total-score pass**, and no automatic “ready” or observed mastery. These local criteria, numbers and accessible response routes require pedagogical review before independent use. Optional timed drills can rehearse calendars or table reading but cannot replace conceptual evidence. Distinguish “authored reference assessment” from “school-reviewed instrument” and “student reached threshold.”
