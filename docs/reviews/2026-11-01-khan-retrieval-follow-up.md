original PV2/PV3 drill and conceptual-form generation. Its
[developer/administration workflow](../exercise-bank.md) documents reproducible
seeds, separate learner sheets/teacher keys/manifests and independent key checks.

- Drill duration is configurable in the local bank; the countdown collects no
  learner responses. Choose a suitable window locally after review.
- Conceptual forms are untimed; their criteria still require models, reasons,
  names and explanations. Each selected export needs freshness, prompt/key,
  scope and access review. Software validation does not establish readiness.
- PV2 stays within 99; PV3 within 999. PVX is not generated.
- The provider searches returned no configurable timer, question generator,
  export, range-filter or progress-import capability. No such provider feature
  is asserted or used.

The statement in the October 11 review that local timed forms are a separate
authoring obligation is historical. The bank and teaching pack now supply draft
implementations; do not rebuild them merely because provider retrieval remains
unresolved. This follow-up checks their documented contracts and linked existing
tasks, not a fresh execution of the generator or classroom delivery.
