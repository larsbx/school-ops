# Instructional patterns: source register

Reviewed 2026-10-25. External materials are referenced, not redistributed.
This is a design bibliography rather than the reviewed practice-resource register.
All example prompts and keys in the [pattern library](../templates/instruction/README.md)
are original school-ops material.

| Source | Observed basis | Adaptation and evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Cambridge Interchange Fifth Edition teacher introduction](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813166/22728/excerpt/9781316622728_excerpt.pdf), printed xi–xiv, xvii–xviii; [detailed notes](interchange-source-notes.md) | Two cycles, communicative tasks and linked practice/teacher materials | Math context and tasks are ours; source is a language-course design, not a Grade 2 math evaluation |
| [Khan Academy: How Should People Practice?](https://blog.khanacademy.org/how-should-people-practice-on-khan-academy/), Kristen DiCerbo, originally 2020, page also displays a 2024 update | Hints during practice, explanations after responses, and self-explanation | Local feedback loop; no imported platform mastery labels or automatic outcome judgments |
| [Khan Academy classroom experience](https://blog.khanacademy.org/meet-the-new-khan-academy-classroom-experience/), page displays May/July 2026 | Mastery-based practice, immediate feedback, videos/articles and worked-example support | Official current feature description, not independent effectiveness evidence or an integration contract |
| [British Council TeachingEnglish: Test teach test](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/professional-development/teachers/teaching-knowledge-database/t-w/test-teach-test) | Initial unaided activity informs teaching, followed by another activity | Adapt short tasks to Grade 2 mathematical components; do not infer causal gains from two responses |
| [Atkinson, Renkl and Merrill (2003), author-university record and abstract](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/transitioning-from-studying-examples-to-solving-problems-effects-/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.95.4.774) | Studies fading worked steps with prompts about underlying principles | Abstract reviewed, not a full-paper audit; far transfer is not guaranteed by fading alone; our Grade 2 ladder is an adaptation |
| [Renkl, Atkinson and Große (2004), publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/B:TRUC.0000021815.74806.f6) | Examines learning with progressively removed solution steps | Preview/abstract only; no claim that one fade direction or fixed rate is best for every learner |
| [IES/WWC: Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning (2007)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/1) | Spacing, alternating worked examples/problems, retrieval and explanatory questions | Guide recommendations have different evidence ratings; the combined local template has not been independently evaluated |
| [Rodger Bybee: The BSCS 5E Instructional Model, Personal Reflections and Contemporary Implications](https://www.nsta.org/sites/default/files/blog/2015/08/sc1408_10.pdf), Science and Children, 2014, pp. 10–13 | Model originator describes engagement, exploration, explanation, elaboration and evaluation across a sequence | Originally science-oriented; our measurement investigation adapts the sequence and adds no standards alignment |

Official Khan help pages redirected to an inaccessible help-center view during
this review; the directly readable Khan-authored blog pages above support the
pattern. No exercise availability or assignment was inferred from those pages.
The BSCS report host was unavailable; the readable original author's NSTA article
provides the cited 5E account. The Interchange structure was checked using the
official Cambridge excerpt and product page.
