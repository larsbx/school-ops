## Verified architecture

1. [Interchange Level 2 Teacher's Edition with Complete Assessment Program,
   Fifth Edition, introduction, printed xi–xiv, xvii–xviii](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813166/22728/excerpt/9781316622728_excerpt.pdf):
   units have two cycles, each with a topic, language focus and communicative
   purpose. Activities vary in order. The sample combines context, dialogue,
   focused language practice, vocabulary, listening, speaking, reading, writing
   and a communicative task. The workbook reinforces taught material; teacher
   support includes teaching notes and keys. Assessment includes oral and
   written work.
2. [Cambridge's Level 2 Student's Book with Digital Pack, ISBN 9781009040693](https://shop.cambridge.org/english/product/2700221662):
   the listed book has sixteen units and four progress checks.

These observations describe the cited edition/components, not every edition.
The source does not establish Grade 2 math topics, time budgets, mathematical
assessment criteria or the checkpoint positions chosen below.

## Our adaptation

[Course design](../curriculum/mathematics/grade-2/course-design.md) illustrates original
math cycles, sixteen optional book modules nested in seven existing units, and four
cumulative checks after modules 4, 8, 12 and 16. Those placements and all tasks
are school-ops design choices. Preserve outcomes and prerequisites, use short
sessions, and assess mathematical reasoning with language support.

No Cambridge pages, dialogue, artwork, exercises, keys, audio or video are copied.
The public references are not a claim of Cambridge endorsement or a completed
teaching-material review. Optional digital production can follow the paper
materials; no Cambridge platform, account or product purchase is required.
