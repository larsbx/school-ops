## Component-specific interpretation (proposed, not psychometric validation)

- **G3R-P1:** Q1 must list the *complete* set A,B and justify completeness, Q2 must reject the universal with D and relevant fields, and Q3 must enumerate A,C,F with stripe evidence. Missing any requires targeted teaching and a **new** independently answered check.
- **G3R-P2:** Q4 accurately states original direction, Q5 correctly states the converse and rejects it with D, and Q7 rejects the other converse with E; **counterexamples are mandatory** even if other responses are correct.
- **G3R-P3:** Q6 explicitly states **R2 and R3** as two premises, Q8 identifies the invalid inference and B's contrary fields, and Q9 says **unknown** with a packet-boundary reason and needed evidence.
