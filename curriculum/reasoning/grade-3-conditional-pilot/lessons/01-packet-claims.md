## Trivium and the actual investigation

- **Grammar:** learn the meanings of **row**, **field**, **every**, **at least one**, **witness** and **counterexample**; identify all values in a named column.
- **Logic:** distinguish proof of `every blue card is dotted` by checking **all** blue rows from the mistaken strategy of checking only A. Distinguish an example that confirms from a counterexample that refutes an all-claim.
- **Rhetoric:** tell a partner which cards were checked and how that establishes or defeats the claim, citing labels/fields rather than aesthetic impressions.

## Proposed 25-minute teaching sequence

| Phase | Minutes | Usable teacher and learner actions |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Retrieve entry | 4 | Read B's Color and Dot fields; allow a pointed/oral answer. Review how to scan six labeled rows. |
| Model a finite claim | 6 | Write “every blue card has a dot.” Locate the **complete set A and B**; compare both Dot entries. Explain why a single matching A is not a full argument. |
| Guided dialectic | 7 | Learners perform P1–P2. Ask “Does every dotted card have to be blue?” Hold back the answer, have learners scan A–F, then contrast R1 with the reversed claim. |
| Rhetorical explanation | 5 | Learners make a two-sentence explanation: “My blue rows are …; their dots are …; therefore …”. Teacher challenges “What about D?” as an alternative *reverse* question, not as a refutation of R1. |
| Independent exit X1 | 3 | Ask: “List all **green numbered** cards and point to both fields. Is card D one of them? Why?” Collect explanation in an accessible response mode. |

**Teacher exit key X1:** **E and F** are the green numbered cards; **D is not** because D is **red** and Number = **no**. Evidence needs an exhaustive green scan (E and F) and both relevant fields, not merely recalling letters.

**Feedback:** If rows are skipped, try highlighting one column at a time. If “every” is proved with only one example, demonstrate the missing-row risk using dotted A versus dotted D for a *different* claim. Recheck the classification with another field before Lesson 2.
