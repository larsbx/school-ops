# Grade 4 reasoning — exceptions, missing facts and repaired rules

**Draft, unregistered, not ready.** This is an original bounded pilot for the first three investigations in the [Grade 4 reasoning scaffold](../../scaffolds/grade-4/reasoning.md), not a course, adopted timetable, or learner result. Canonical course/unit/lesson/outcome/assessment identifiers are unassigned. P1–P4 and E/X/G/S labels are local editorial references only.

The [Grade 3 reasoning pilot](../grade-3-conditional-pilot/README.md) tests one-way rules and converses within a closed packet. This increment adds explicit override precedence, independent missing facts, and minimally repaired rules; it does not assume Grade 3 was taught. Grade 2 mathematics and Islamic studies are untouched. Primary art is dialectic within the Trivium; Boolean enumeration is a reasoning representation, not a new Quadrivium attainment claim. Music is excluded.

## Observable targets and evidence

| Target | Observable work | Proposed reference evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Apply a two-condition route, an alternative route, and a closure veto; cite which condition controls the result. | S1–S3: every decision and governing fact correct. |
| P2 | Distinguish a known negative from unknown; exhibit compatible completions that change an answer or explain why none can. | S4–S6: correct result and completion/reason for each. |
| P3 | Test a universal claim by giving a valid counterexample; distinguish necessary from sufficient within the supplied rule. | S7–S8: explicit legal row and reason for each. |
| P4 | Repair an overbroad rule without excluding an allowed alternative or bypassing a veto, and defend the repair against positive/negative examples. | S9–S10: equivalent wording and both checks. |

Entry E1–E4 and distinct rechecks are in [learner entry](entry-learner.md) and [teacher entry](entry-teacher.md). E1/E2 gate episode 1; E3 gates episode 2; E4 gates episode 3. Episode 4 depends on the exits of 1–3. Reteach and recheck missing components; do not promote from age, attendance, or an average score. No assumption of prior enrollment.

## Material and sequence

- [Fictional rule and card packet](rule-packet.md), [four teaching episodes](lessons.md), [exit and guided teacher key](guided-teacher.md).
- [Guided learner tasks](guided-learner.md) are coached rehearsal. [Reference learner tasks](draft-assessments/reference-learner.md) and [reference teacher key](draft-assessments/reference-teacher.md) are public exposed examples, not secure independent evidence.
- Four provisional 25-minute episodes and a separate untimed reference rehearsal (plan for 25 minutes, allow continuation). Materials: printable text cards, blank yes/no/? table, pencils or AAC. Read aloud, offer large-print or screen-reader linear rows, accept pointing followed by dictated explanation. Never encode information solely by color; do not highlight decisive cells during independent checks. Preserve the same logical demand in each mode.
