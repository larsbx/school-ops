# Guided rehearsal (draft)

Use the [rule packet](rule-packet.md). Explain with the decisive field(s).

**G1.** Compare A and B. Why do their decisions differ?

**G2.** Compare C and D. Does a helper pass always admit?

**G3.** Complete E twice. What decisions result? What can we conclude before the missing value arrives?

**G4.** Explain why F and I can each be decided despite missing facts.

**G5.** Is having a partner necessary for admission? Use one complete card.

**G6.** Is being open sufficient? Use a complete counterexample.

**G7.** A proposed rule says “admit if open and token.” Find an excluded admissible card and an wrongly admitted card.

**G8.** A proposed rule says “admit if (open and token and partner) or helper.” Find a wrongly admitted card; repair the wording.
