# Four draft teaching episodes

Use the [rule packet](rule-packet.md). Each episode has 25 provisional minutes; the teacher uses the separate guide for exits. Not an adopted schedule.

## Episode 1 — Two routes and a veto (P1)

| Phase | Activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grammar: | Name token, partner, helper pass, closed, both, inclusive or, veto. Read A, B and C linearly. | 5 |
| Logic: | Build two routes with cards. Test A, B, C and then D. Cover both routes with a closure strip for D. Ask what changes if D opens. | 10 |
| Rhetoric: | Partners explain C and D using “although …, the rule …”; switch roles. Guided G1–G2. | 7 |
| Exit | Independently answer X1. | 3 |

**X1.** New card K: T=0, P=1, H=1, C=1. Decide and cite the overriding fact.

## Episode 2 — Unknown is not no (P2)

| Phase | Activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grammar: | Define completion, compatible, unknown and undetermined. Write ? as a missing entry, not a third truth value. | 5 |
| Logic: | Replace E's ? by 0 and 1 in separate rows. Compare F and G: enumerate missing fields or show why a fixed fact settles all possibilities. | 10 |
| Rhetoric: | Explain to a partner why a missing fact sometimes matters and sometimes does not. Guided G3–G4. | 7 |
| Exit | Independently answer X2. | 3 |

**X2.** K: T=0, P=?, H=1, C=0. Is the decision undetermined? Justify for both completions.

## Episode 3 — Test necessary and sufficient (P3)

| Phase | Activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grammar: | Necessary = must hold for every admitted card; sufficient = guarantees admission. Distinguish claim and counterexample. | 5 |
| Logic: | Test “a token is necessary” with C; test “a helper pass is sufficient” with D. Check why open is necessary but not sufficient (B). | 10 |
| Rhetoric: | Exchange a claimed rule and a counterexample row; explain which half of the claim fails. Guided G5–G6. | 7 |
| Exit | Independently answer X3. | 3 |

**X3.** Refute “every open card admits” using a new complete row not in A–J.

## Episode 4 — Repair the rule (P4)

| Phase | Activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grammar: | Read “open AND (both token and partner OR helper)” aloud with brackets; identify scope of the veto. | 5 |
| Logic: | Compare two faulty summaries: open AND token; (open AND token AND partner) OR helper. Use A–D to test exclusion and bypass. Repair the summaries. | 10 |
| Rhetoric: | Explain the repair to a partner who checks one permitted and one vetoed case. Guided G7–G8. | 7 |
| Exit | Independently answer X4. | 3 |

**X4.** Rewrite “any helper pass admits” into the complete correct rule. Show a permitted non-helper row and a vetoed helper row.

If an exit is incomplete, use a different row, explicitly model the missed step, and administer a fresh equivalent exit without cues. Do not advance dependent work based on attendance.
