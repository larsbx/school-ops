# Guided learner tasks — draft, exposed practice

Use the [newly invented source packet](source-packet.md). Explain your reasoning. Reading aloud, dictated answers and pointing to line labels are allowed; these are not independent assessment or evidence of mastery.

**G1.** In "Please recheck the drawing tomorrow," what does *re-* mean? Does the sentence tell us exactly when the earlier check happened?

**G2.** In A2, choose (a) opened a folded map or (b) folded it again for *unfolded*. Explain from context.

**G3.** Put four events in line order: noticed tear, traced route, edited copy with reminder, did not walk route that day.

**G4.** Arrange these sentences to make a paragraph: (a) They did not walk it that day. (b) Noor noticed a torn corner. (c) She traced the route. (d) She added a staircase and a request to check. Explain whether this proves verification.

**G5.** Eli says the old map is useless (A3). Give a line and concrete detail against this conclusion. Does that prove the old map is safe?

**G6.** Calculate net book changes for both B1 and B2. Give two different Monday additions/removals that produce the same net change.

**G7.** Explain in two sentences one change Noor made to the copy and one limit on what can be concluded. Cite two exact A labels.

**G8.** Correct this memo for a classmate: "Noor verified the new route because she wrote a check note." Include a line ID and distinguish quotation from your own paraphrase.

The [teacher key](teacher-guide.md) is separate; do not present it as new unseen tasks.
