# Learner entry work — draft, ungraded

Use [fictional Text A](source-packet.md). Read/listen, respond orally, point, order cards, dictate or write. This is starting-skill evidence, not a placement verdict.

**E1.** In A1, who noticed what? Give the words that show your answer.

**E2.** Order these events and name the source lines: (a) added a staircase to the copy, (b) noticed the torn corner, (c) traced a route on a clean sheet.

**E3.** Which A line supports the claim "The old map includes a staircase absent from the copy"? Point to the detail.

**E4.** Choose the accurate restatement of A6 and explain: (i) "They walked the route immediately." (ii) "They did not walk the route that day." Does either sentence tell what happens next week?
