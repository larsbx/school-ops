## A — The folded library map (fictional narrative)

**A1.** On Monday, Noor noticed that the library's paper map had a torn corner.

**A2.** She unfolded the map on the reading table, placed a clean sheet beneath it, and traced the route to the art room.

**A3.** Eli said, "The copied map is clearer, so the old map is useless."

**A4.** Noor replied, "The old one shows the staircase that the copy leaves out."

**A5.** At lunch, Noor drew the staircase onto the copy and wrote, "Check this route with a teacher."

**A6.** They did not walk the route that day.

## B — The book-table note (invented snapshots; not a loan record)

**B1.** Monday: the reading table held 12 books before a visit and 9 afterward.

**B2.** Tuesday: the reading table held 10 books before a visit and 11 afterward.

**B3.** A sign said, "Please return borrowed books to the bin."

**B4.** The recorder did not count how many books were added or removed during either visit.

**Reading guard:** A5 is a *request* to check, not a completed route check; A6 establishes only what did not happen *that day*. B1 and B2 identify changes in observed totals, not which people borrowed books, which books were returned to a bin, or the number of additions/removals.
