## Provisional decision rules (owner review needed)

- **P1: S1 and S2.** Both require figurative/literal boundary and a non-inference.
- **P2: S3, S4 and S5.** All require correct attributed observation, inference classification, and view reconciliation.
- **P3: S6, S7 and S8.** All require timeline or place-aware warranted comparison and at least one properly named gap.
- **P4: S9 and S10.** Both require clear audience-facing claim, attributed source line(s), a warranted counterpoint and uncertainty.

Record each outcome as **evidence present / partial / not yet observed**, not a numerical mastery label; a partial answer triggers precise feedback and a *fresh equivalent, unseen* form after reteaching. This public form is unsuitable for secure high-stakes decisions. Teacher review must inspect potential alternative interpretations and inequitable language burdens. Unknown schedule, actual learners, reading access and oral/scribe equivalence remain explicit blockers.
