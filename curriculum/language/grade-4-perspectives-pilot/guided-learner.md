# Guided learner tasks G1–G8

**Draft instructional work, not a secure assessment.** Use the [original fictional packet](source-packet.md). Respond orally, in writing, with line-label cards, or using an exact scribe; source identifiers must remain visible.

**G1.** Read A3. Is the hallway literally eating footsteps? Say what the image helps a reader imagine, and one event it does *not* establish.

**G2.** Rewrite A3 in literal words, keeping only what A1–A5 permit you to say. Identify whether your new line is a quotation or paraphrase.

**G3.** List what Leena actually saw and did not see. Give two line identifiers and explain why her account cannot establish who moved the folders.

**G4.** Noah announces “Someone cleared everything.” Identify one observation, one inference, and a line that contradicts the strongest version of his claim.

**G5.** Build a cautious three-step sequence with A2, C2, B4. Mark what the log **reports** and what two observers **say they saw**. Do not name the mover.

**G6.** Assess “Noah stole the folders” and “The folders were reported on the east shelf at 08:17.” For each, give a warrant or state exactly what is missing. Can the three cards prove the final inventory?

**G7.** Rewrite: “Noah saw empty shelves, so somebody stole every folder.” Give two attributed sentences and one sentence setting a limit, for a reader unfamiliar with the room.

**G8.** A skeptical reader says, “But the room log could be mistaken.” Give a respectful reply that distinguishes what the log records from what can be independently confirmed. Make a precise revision rather than dismissing the objection.
