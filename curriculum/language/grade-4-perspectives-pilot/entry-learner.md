# Entry checks — learner sheet (E1–E4)

**Draft, untimed, not secure assessment.** Use writing, speech, pointing to line labels or a scribe. Teacher answers and support decisions are in a separate file. These independent prompts use situations other than the blue-folder source packet.

**E1 — Record or guess.** A door notice says, “Studio opens at nine.” A learner says, “It will probably be busy.” Which is directly stated in the notice? Which is a prediction? Quote or point to the evidence.

**E2 — Language, not an event.** A writer says, “The rain drummed on the roof.” Is it necessary that a real drummer was on the roof? Explain the difference between the words and the event they suggest.

**E3 — Textual warrant.** A fictional library card reads, “On Tuesday, five maps were returned to the desk.” A friend says, “The maps were returned by Omar.” Is the friend's claim established? Give the exact phrase that supports your answer or explain what is missing.

**E4 — Coherent explanation.** Put these two statements into a useful order, then add a sentence that names what is still unknown: (a) “The window was open at noon.” (b) “The room log says the window was closed at 11:30.” Do not invent the person responsible.
