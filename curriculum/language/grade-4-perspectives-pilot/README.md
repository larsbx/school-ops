# Grade 4 language — perspective, figurative meaning and warranted revision

**Authored draft / unregistered / not ready.** This is a proposal, not a registered course, teaching-ready lesson, adopted timetable, independent assessment, or learner result. Formal course, unit, lesson, outcome and assessment IDs remain **unassigned** until the owner approves a scope. Labels G4-LANG-P1–P4 and E/G/X/S are local authoring references, never canonical IDs. Four illustrative 25-minute episodes are **not** a time allocation decision.

This pilot extends the [Grade 4 language scaffold](../../scaffolds/grade-4/language.md), following—not assuming mastery of—the [Grade 3 evidence-writing pilot](../grade-3-evidence-writing-pilot/README.md). It adds **conflicting partial narrators, nonliteral language and revisions for a skeptical absent reader** instead of copying Grade 3's word-part work and numerical-claim tasks. Original fictional texts only: [paired narrators and a partial record](source-packet.md); no outside citations or redistribution rights are assumed. This is Trivium work (grammar, logic, rhetoric), not a claimed arithmetic/geometry/astronomy lesson; music is excluded.

## Proposed observable outcomes and evidence

| Local label | Observable work under stated conditions | Worked/reference evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G4-LANG-P1 | Interpret literal versus figurative sentences; explain why a metaphor cannot by itself establish an event | G1/G2, X1, S1–S2 |
| G4-LANG-P2 | Attribute a line to the speaker and distinguish observation, report, inference and knowledge limit; compare partial viewpoints | G3/G4, X2, S3–S5 |
| G4-LANG-P3 | Construct a bounded event claim using at least two correctly identified records, and reject an overclaim with a counterexample or uncertainty | G5/G6, X3, S6–S8 |
| G4-LANG-P4 | Revise a coherent paragraph for an absent reader: claim, precise citation/paraphrase, qualified inference and counterpoint | G7/G8, X4, S9–S10 |

**Evidence is noncompensatory:** P1–P4 are checked separately; a well-written paragraph cannot compensate for a false citation, and oral fluency alone is not evidence of source reasoning. Fixed exemplars are **publicly exposed**; do not reuse as secure unseen mastery items. No proficiency or grade placement is inferred from task completion.

## Entry, instruction, feedback and assessments

Use [four entry probes](entry-learner.md) with [complete teacher answers, micro-remediation and fresh rechecks](entry-teacher.md). E1/E3 precede evidence comparison; E2 precedes literal/figurative distinctions; E4 is retaught before audience revision. An incomplete prerequisite leads to targeted instruction and a *different* recheck; a learner is never barred from supported practice by a grade label. Never remove text/response supports merely to make a test seem independent.

The [teaching sequences](lessons.md) include exact teacher prompts, Trivium actions, independent X1–X4 and feedback moves, with illustrative minutes. [Eight guided learner tasks](guided-learner.md) have a [separate teacher key](guided-teacher.md) containing model reasons and exit keys. A distinct fictional [public reference packet and ten tasks](draft-assessments/reference-learner.md) have a [separate full teacher key and rubric](draft-assessments/reference-teacher.md). The two fictional situations are not evidence of actual historical events or persons.

**Permitted access modes (subject to local review):** text enlargement, screen-reader-ready plain Markdown, read-aloud of *all* source words and line identifiers, neutral vocabulary glossary, speech, pointing-to-line cards, scribing or writing. Dictation may capture a learner's exact wording, not supply inference; cueing a correct source invalidates independent evidence and calls for a fresh form. Record individual accommodations, scores and writing only privately.
