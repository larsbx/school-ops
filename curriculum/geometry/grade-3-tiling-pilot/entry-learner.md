# Grade 3 geometry — E1–E4 entry check (learner copy; draft)

Untimed, with oral, tactile or large-print alternatives. `#` means one equal-size filled unit square. Give a demonstration or explanation, not a guessed number.

**E1 Count tiles.** Rows:
```text
##
##
```
How many filled squares? How do you know you counted each once?

**E2 Add connected segments.** A straight line is made from a **3-unit** segment and then a **2-unit** segment, joined end to end without overlap. How long is the full line? Show the unit intervals.

**E3 Side or corner?** Two tile pairs are supplied in words: pair A shares **one complete side**, pair B touches at **only a corner**. Which pair hides a full edge from the outside boundary? Explain using tiles or gestures.

**E4 Rearrange without loss.** Build **six** tiles into **two equal rows**. How many in each row, and why does rearranging not change the total?

Do not write a pupil's name here. An unmet gate leads to separate reteaching, not a numerical average.
