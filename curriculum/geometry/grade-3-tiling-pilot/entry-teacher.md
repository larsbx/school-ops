# Grade 3 geometry — entry teacher key and routes (draft)

[Separate learner E1–E4](entry-learner.md). These are *independent prerequisite gates*, not one combined pass score; record actual pupil evidence only in private systems.

| Gate | Full correct model and evidence | Failure and accessible remediation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | **4 filled squares**: 2+2; count each tile exactly once. | If counting uncertain, physically move/mark counted tiles; independently recheck with a new 3-tile and 5-tile patch. |
| **E2** | **5 length units** (3+2=5) since intervals join without overlap; count segments rather than endpoints. | Align two strips, trace intervals and retry 2+4. |
| **E3** | **Pair A**: its shared full side becomes internal; B's corner-only touch hides **no** whole side. | Feel/see edge-to-edge versus corner-to-corner contacts; retry new arrangements, not memorized labels. |
| **E4** | **3 per row**, 3+3=6; all six tiles remain present when rearranged. | Construct/recount two equal groups with tactile tiles, then recheck with an equivalent novel arrangement. |

**Decision:** E1 gates area, E2+E3 gate exposed-side perimeter, E4 gates shape rearrangement; no skill is inferred solely because another passed. Read aloud, scribe, or use raised tiles while preserving mathematical explanations. Reteach missing prerequisites before the associated lessons; no automatic promotion or grading of family practice.
