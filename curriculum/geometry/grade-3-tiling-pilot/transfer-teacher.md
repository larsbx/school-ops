# Transfer clinic T1–T5 — teacher key, routing and review (draft)

**Use:** original supplementary *teaching* and diagnostic examples for [T1–T5 learner tasks](transfer-learner.md). **Not ready**, not independently validated student assessment or a new canonical outcome. The key must not be linked from the learner copy. Source: author-created equal-square diagrams and verbal scenarios; no externally licensed content or measured real site is incorporated.

These tasks deliberately test *boundary conditions of definitions*, not repeat G1–G7. All fixed tasks and answers are public. Keep individual work, accommodations and results out of GitHub.

| Task | Exact model answer and proof | Diagnostic next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1** | Eight filled tiles = **8 square units**. Outer 3+3+3+3 = **12 length units**; the internal square opening exposes **4 length units**. **Every exposed side = 16 length units**. Counting only the outside outline excludes the four edges facing the opening. An eight-tile ring has eight full shared sides, so `4×8−2×8=16` is an independent audit. | If pupil says 12 for “every exposed side,” physically trace the inner opening with a finger, raised strip or pointer. Recheck with a **new** single central opening in a larger model; do not reuse T1 as a claimed independent assessment. |
| **T2** | A: **2 square units, 8 length units** (no shared full sides); A is **not side-connected**. B: **2 square units, 6 length units** (one full shared side hides two tile edges); B is **side-connected**. Only **B** meets “one region joined by full tile sides.” | Have learner compare full-side contact, corner-only contact and a small visible gap. A corner can touch without any common full side; do not confuse “touching somewhere” with the stated adjacency criterion. |
| **T3** | Hook: **6 square units** and **14 length units**. There are **5 shared full sides**: 2 in top row, 1 in bottom row, and 2 between successive rows; `4×6−2×5=14`. Rectangle **6 square units, 10 length units** (7 joins). Strip **6 square units, 14 length units** (5 joins). Of the **three given** layouts, rectangle has the shortest exposed boundary, but testing three cannot prove a universal optimum over all connected six-square layouts. | If a universal claim is made, ask what list of possibilities has been exhausted or what general proof covers them. The learner may propose enumerating all valid figures, but no enumeration proof is supplied here. |
| **T4** | Both drawings contain **6 square units**. The 2-by-3 boundary is `3+2+3+2=10` **length units**; the 3-by-2 boundary is `2+3+2+3=10` **length units**. Changing orientation keeps tile sizes and shared sides the same. | Rotate a 2-by-3 tactile block or paper sheet; require a re-count of both area and exposed sides, not “they look alike.” |
| **T5** | C and D each contain **4 tiles**. C occupies a 2 cm-by-2 cm square: **4 cm²** area, **8 cm** outer boundary. D occupies a 4 cm-by-4 cm square: **16 cm²** area, **16 cm** outer boundary. Four large tiles each cover four 1 cm² squares. Tile count remains four, but **tile size differs**. Equal counts imply equal physical area **only for equal-area tiles**. | Make a paper 1 cm-by-1 cm tile and a 2 cm-by-2 cm tile, each with unit-square subdivisions. Do not treat multiplying area by four as already mastered; reteach physical square-centimetre measurement before repeating with new sizes. |

## Relation to core local goals

- **P1 exposed boundary:** T1 differentiates outer versus *all* exposed edges when a hole exists; T2 tests shared-side versus corner contact; T3 and T4 independently trace full sides. Holes and non-side-connected examples are **optional extensions**: the core G/R forms have no holes.
- **P2 area:** T1–T4 require count of equal-unit squares; T5 separates number of tiles from measured physical area. T5 is an **optional stretch**, not an adopted grade expectation or a new core pass requirement.
- **P3 angles:** Remains covered by the original [G5/G7](guided-learner.md) and [R6/R7](draft-assessments/reference-learner.md); this clinic does not invent redundant angle drills.
- **P4 defensible inference:** T3 requires a *bounded* comparison and refusal of unsupported universal claims; T5 identifies a missing equal-size premise.

**Trivium-rich optional 25-minute clinic, not a scheduled lesson:** 4 minutes grammar (state area, side, corner, exposed/outer, square-centimetre units), 7 minutes logic (T1/T2 side and hole counterexamples), 6 minutes model-and-check (T3/T4 side counts), 5 minutes rhetoric (explain why three tests do not prove “every”), 3 minutes exit check (teacher chooses *new* altered diagrams to identify missing premise). T5 only after unit-size readiness; replace another extension, do not compress required access time. The claimed “25 minutes” is a suggested structure subject to owner approval, not a timetable.

## Noncompensatory response routing

| Observed *task response*, not inferred learner trait | Check, support, and recheck |
| --- | --- |
| Confuses area and boundary | Trace one square of area and one exposed edge length separately; learner names square units versus length units; independently retry on a new rectangle. |
| Omits inside opening | Follow both T1 loops with tactile strips; ask whether instruction means outside outline or **all** exposed sides; retry on a different hole configuration. |
| Treats corner as shared edge | Physically separate/rejoin two tiles, contrast a full shared side with a point; recheck on different adjacent cells. |
| Treats three observations as universal proof | Ask learner to state the quantified domain (“these three” versus “all”); request a valid bounded conclusion, with new options for later independent evidence. |
| Assumes tile count fixes physical area | Compare side lengths and count 1 cm² subdivisions; do not demand T5 without that prerequisite. |

**Access and assessment controls:** Accept oral, tactile, enlarged or scribed responses and the same geometric justification. Do not grade handwriting, dexterity, vision or time. These public items are formative only; do not substitute them for an unseen independent assessment, clinical assessment of need or teacher-approved new criterion. A teacher should inspect all models, unit language, candidate age suitability and actual supports before use.

**Review blockers:** approve whether holes/disconnected patterns and unit scaling are suitable extensions, distinguish total exposed boundary from external outline in local teaching language, verify arithmetic and diagrams, agree actual time/materials/access, and commission an unseen independently reviewed check if required. Existing Grade 2 **C-MATH-001** and its open units are not changed. The 13×8 matrix and Islamic studies source/translation/hadith/fiqh gates are untouched.
