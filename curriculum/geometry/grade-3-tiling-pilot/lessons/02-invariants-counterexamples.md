# Episode 2 — same boundary need not mean same area

**Draft**, 25 illustrative minutes; Geometry/arithmetic P1/P2/P4. Gate E1–E4 evidence or a separate recheck. Printed/tactile original arrangements.

**Grammar:** area, perimeter, *invariant*, counterexample, and the logical force of “always.” **Logic:** 1×4 has area4/perimeter10; 2×3 has area6/perimeter10. A single constructed case refutes “equal boundary implies equal area.” Conversely a 1×6 and 2×3 both have area6 but perimeters14 and10. **Rhetoric:** present claim, both numerical measurements, reason, and a corrective statement.

| 3 | Exit: “If equal area then equal perimeter”—always true? | **No**, exhibit 2×3 and 1×6; explain in words. |
