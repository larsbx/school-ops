# Episode 3 — angle direction rather than ray length

**Draft**, illustrative 25 minutes; Geometry P3. Recheck square-corner recognition at start. Equipment: safe two-strip hinge (paper or tactile) or descriptions of compass directions; no sharp tools or protractor required.

**Common construction:** The vertex is O; the **first ray points east**. Compare the **smaller anticlockwise turn** to second rays northeast, north and northwest. North gives the square-corner *right angle*.

**Grammar:** vertex, ray, direction, right-angle corner, narrower/wider turn. **Logic:** northeast is narrower, north right, northwest wider, independent of rays' printed lengths. Extension of a ray doesn't rotate it. **Rhetoric:** identify baseline/turn direction and explain why a claimed “longer arm = bigger angle” is mistaken.

| Minutes | Action | Evidence |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4 | Locate O, east baseline, square corner. | Recognize shared vertex. |
| 6 | Pivot one strip successively to NE, N and NW. | Classify smaller/right/larger turns. |
| 7 | Solve [G5/G7](../guided-learner.md) with direction explanations. | Distinguish 2-unit vs 5-unit ray lengths from opening. |
| 5 | Rotate the whole sheet, maintaining the hinge opening. | Sheet orientation does **not** change angle. |
| 3 | Exit: extend northern ray but hold directions fixed. | **No** change to the east–north angle. |

If the learner uses endpoint distance, cover the ray tips and pivot the hinge at O. Accept verbal/tactile explanations, not just fine-motor drawing. [Full key](../guided-teacher.md).
