# Episode 1 — trace exposed boundaries

**Draft**; illustrative **25 minutes**; primary Geometry with arithmetic, P1/P2; requires E1–E3 or reteaching. Materials: six equal tiles/raised outlines, blank grid, broad marker as accessible. All figures are original.

**Grammar:** define tile, unit square, exposed side, shared internal side, *square* versus *length* units. **Logic:** two squares joined side-to-side expose **6**, not 8 edges: their shared side is internal for both squares, so 4+4−2=6. **Rhetoric:** narrate a boundary walk and explain the distinction to a partner, orally or in a labeled model.
