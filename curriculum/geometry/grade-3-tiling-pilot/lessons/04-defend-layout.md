# Episode 4 — defend a constrained design

**Draft**, illustrative 25 minutes; Geometry/arithmetic P2/P4 with review of P1. Proceed only after the relevant prerequisites and prior checks. Model brief is *fictional*: six equal floor tiles, side-connected with no gaps, choose between **2×3** and **1×6**; minimize **outside boundary**, not area.

**Grammar:** interpret “exactly six square units” and “shorter outside boundary” as different specifications. **Logic:** area6 holds in both options; boundary10 for 2×3 versus14 for 1×6; these data prove only the better of **these two options**, not globally shortest among all possible shapes. **Rhetoric:** state a recommendation, cite both quantities, and answer the objection “long strip covers more floor.”
