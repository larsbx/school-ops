# R1–R10 exposed reference form — learner copy (draft)

**NOT a secure independent assessment.** This fixed example and its key are public. Suggested untimed conditions only; owner must approve actual administration, new unseen form and materials. Oral, tactile, enlarged or scribed responses may preserve the same geometric criterion. Each `#` is an equal filled unit square with side one length unit, `.` is empty. Use **square units** for area, **length units** for perimeter. Explain where asked.

**R1** Rows `### / ###`: find area and outside boundary, showing an exposed-edge count.

**R2** Row `####`: find area and perimeter, with units.

**R3** Rows `##. / ###`: find area and perimeter. Explain why a shared full side is not an outer side.

**R4** Row `######`: find area and perimeter. Compare area and boundary to R1.

**R5** Three rows of `###` (filled 3×3 square): find area and perimeter.

**R6** At O start with a ray pointing east. Second rays point northeast, north, northwest; classify the smaller anticlockwise turns as less/equal/greater than a right angle. Explain using the square corner.

**R7** A ray east and a ray north form an angle. Both rays are lengthened without changing directions. A pupil claims the angle doubled. Correct the claim and explain a test.

**R8** R1 versus R2: show both areas and perimeters and decide “equal perimeter necessarily gives equal area.”

**R9** R1 versus R4: show both areas and perimeters and decide “equal area necessarily gives equal perimeter.”

**R10** Two squares join along one full side. A pupil says their outside boundary is 4+4=8. Correct the boundary **with units** and explain.
