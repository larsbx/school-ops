# Entry evidence — learner

**Draft, untimed and unscored publicly exposed practice.** A teacher can read the questions aloud; answer by labeled objects, speech or coordinate lists. These do not assume prior course completion. Use the [coordinate rules and cards](coordinate-cards.md). A skill not shown must be taught and checked again on a different example.

**E1. Equal-square count.** Cells `(1,1), (2,1), (2,2)` are filled. State the area in **square units**. Show why a different arrangement of these same three square tiles still has that area.

**E2. Coordinate order.** Locate `(2,4)`. Which number names the column, and which names the row? Give the position of a cell in column 5, row 0.

**E3. Side contact.** Do `(0,0)` and `(1,1)` share a full side? Do `(0,0)` and `(1,0)`? Give one reason using the coordinate differences.

**E4. Mirror pairs.** The V mirror lies between columns 2 and 3. Match each column with its partner: 0, 1, 2. Explain why a square does not disappear under reflection.
