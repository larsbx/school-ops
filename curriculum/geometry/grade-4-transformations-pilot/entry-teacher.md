# Entry key, targeted reteaching and **different** rechecks

**Draft teacher-only guidance; not readiness certification.** Score each prerequisite individually as **shown / not yet shown**. For each missing criterion, model with labeled blocks, then remove the answer example and use the *different* recheck below. Keep read-aloud, tactile grid, scribe and alternate communication supports without telling the learner the coordinate/reason.

| Check | Complete answer and observable criterion | Targeted teaching | Different recheck (answer) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | **3 square units**; reordering the same three nonoverlapping equal squares cannot change their count/area. Require count, unit and conservation reason. | Physically move three identical square tokens without adding, removing or overlapping. | Four squares `(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(2,1)`: **4 square units**; each rearrangement still has 4 because no tile added/removed. |
| E2 | `(2,4)`: **column 2, row 4**. Other cell is `(5,0)`; require both coordinate order and correct second coordinate. | Trace column label first and row second on embossed or printed edges. | Column 3, row 1 gives **(3,1)**; in `(4,2)`, 4 names the column and 2 the row. |
| E3 | `(0,0),(1,1)` touch only at a **corner**, no shared full side; `(0,0),(1,0)` share one full side, since only column changes by 1. Require both and reason. | Pair square tiles side-to-side and corner-to-corner; trace exposed sides. | `(2,2),(2,3)` **share a side**; `(2,2),(3,3)` **only a corner**. |
| E4 | Pair **0↔5, 1↔4, 2↔3**; a reflected cell has exactly one image cell; require all three correspondences and the one-to-one explanation. | Fold paper at the column boundary; count equally spaced unit-square centers. | Under H, pair rows **0↔5, 1↔4, 2↔3**; a cell retains exactly one image. |

Do **not** treat an assisted demonstration as independent evidence. New evidence is collected only in an authorized private system. Entry decisions and any alternate rechecks require human pedagogical judgment; grade labels imply nothing.
