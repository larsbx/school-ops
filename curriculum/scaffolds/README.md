# K–12 class × subject planning scaffold

**Planning only:** 13 grade-level indexes and 104 grade-specific subject files. None are automatically registered courses, completed units, adopted standards, teaching calendars or learner evidence. [Music is excluded](../../docs/decisions/0005-no-music.md).

Trivium (grammar, logic and rhetoric) is active in all files. Mathematics, geometry and observational astronomy use the chosen mathematical arts. Language, logic, natural science and history/geography are provisional subject tracks. **Islamic studies is now included for every grade**, subject to the [Islamic studies source and teaching guide](islamic-studies-guide.md). Its individual course scopes, authentic sources and assessments still require review. The real [Grade 2 mathematics course](../mathematics/grade-2/README.md) takes precedence.

## Promotion gates

1. Demonstrate actual entry prerequisites, rather than assuming skills from grade labels.
2. Agree each course's subject boundary, outcomes, reviewed resources and teaching time before assigning new stable course IDs.
3. Author real unit and lesson tasks, assessment designs and criteria using the [curriculum model](../../docs/curriculum-model.md).
4. Register only actual drafted courses in the [curriculum course index](../README.md); these 104 planning files are not course records.
5. Review each promotion through a pull request, preserve private learner records, and distinguish teaching readiness from mastery.
6. For Islamic studies, distinguish Qur'an, translation, hadith, scholarly interpretation, historical inference and contested fiqh positions. Do not claim unaudited authenticity, issue unsupported legal rulings or assess personal sincerity.

See [prerequisite-coverage issue #11](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/issues/11); these scaffolds do not complete its independent coverage audit. Prioritize finishing the existing Grade 2 mathematics progression.
