## Proposed entry evidence

Ask the learner to demonstrate **justify area and perimeter** from a prior planning bin, using an example and explanation. Do not assume it was taught.

## Proposed sequence (not adopted units)

| Order | Investigation | Observable demonstration to develop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Represent decimal place value | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 2 | Compare fractional quantities | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 3 | Solve multistep measure problems | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |

The ordering and difficulty are proposals, not grade-based prerequisite claims. The following draft pilot authors local outcomes, prerequisite checks, tasks and provisional criteria for the first three investigations as a bounded exact-measure study; other scope and progression decisions remain open.

## Proposed culminating performance

**Candidate task:** Defend a fraction-to-decimal comparison of measures.

**Assessment gap:** The draft pilot supplies exposed tasks, administration conditions, supports, keys and proposed criteria; fresh reviewed independent forms and adopted criteria remain missing. Proposed dimensions: accurate representation and naming; justified reason or counterexample; and an explanation another learner could check. These are **not** assessment results.

## Trivium in this subject

- **Grammar:** use number, quantity and notation precisely.
- **Logic:** investigate quantitative relationships and counterexamples.
- **Rhetoric:** communicate a checkable mathematical explanation.
