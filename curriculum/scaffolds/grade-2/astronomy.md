# Grade 2 — Astronomy

- Record kind: **planning scaffold**, not a course, unit, lesson or assessment
- Course ID and outcome IDs: **unassigned**; do not reserve before scope approval
- Teaching status: **not ready**; no *standalone astronomy subject course* lesson or assessment is claimed as authored
- Intended audience: Grade 2 (skills must be checked)
- Primary study: Astronomy
- Session length, frequency, dates and access: **not selected**
- Framework: Trivium and selected Quadrivium arts; music excluded

## Proposed entry evidence

Ask the learner to demonstrate **contrast clocks and sky events** from a prior planning bin, using an example and explanation. Do not assume it was taught.

## Proposed sequence (not authored units)

| Order | Investigation | Observable demonstration to develop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Describe moon appearances | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 2 | Compare elapsed observation dates | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 3 | Identify repeating patterns | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |

The ordering and difficulty are proposals, not grade-based prerequisite claims. Real outcomes, prerequisite dependencies, tasks and success criteria must be authored after checking entry work.

## Proposed culminating performance

**Candidate task:** Create a moon-observation table and state what missing dates imply.

**Assessment gap:** Specify actual tasks, administration conditions, supports, teacher key or model responses, and threshold criteria. Proposed dimensions: accurate representation and naming; justified reason or counterexample; and an explanation another learner could check. These are **not** assessment results.

## Trivium in this subject

- **Grammar:** use sky objects, dates and measurement terms precisely.
- **Logic:** investigate observations versus hypotheses.
- **Rhetoric:** communicate an explained dated observation.

## Existing Grade 2 mathematics astronomy strand (different record type)

The existing **draft** [C-MATH-001 Unit 5 sky-records pilot](../../mathematics/grade-2/units/sky-records/README.md) now contains four authored lesson drafts, an entry check, learner practice, a blank **real-observation** template and separate reference assessment/key for **existing course outcomes O-MATH-001-010 and 011**. This does **not** register an independent Grade 2 astronomy course, supply authentic lunar records, establish classroom readiness or replace this scaffold's unselected standalone scope. The synthetic May/March datasets are exercises, **not** historical astronomical observations. Genuine sourced sky-record evidence, safety/access and teacher review remain blocked; [course model](../../../docs/curriculum-model.md) applies.
