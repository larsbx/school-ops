## Proposed entry evidence

Check whether the learner distinguishes attribution, reliability evidence and bare assertion.

## Proposed sequence (not authored units)

| Order | Investigation | Observable demonstration to develop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Qur'anic transmission and carefully defined recitation variants | Define selected terms, identify actual source types, and explain a relevant claim or reason using reviewed evidence. |
| 2 | Hadith classifications: ṣaḥīḥ, ḥasan, ḍaʿīf and uncertainty | Define selected terms, identify actual source types, and explain a relevant claim or reason using reviewed evidence. |
| 3 | Sīrah and historical source criticism | Define selected terms, identify actual source types, and explain a relevant claim or reason using reviewed evidence. |

These investigations are planning hypotheses, not assertions about learner prerequisites or religious practice. Select and review actual source passages, translations, hadith reports and scholarly interpretations before teaching.

## Proposed culminating performance

**Candidate task:** Audit a cited report's provenance and explain which authenticity claims have supporting evidence and which remain undetermined.

**Assessment gap:** Author actual tasks, citations, administration conditions, permitted supports, teacher keys or model answers and objective success criteria. Check accurate representation, evidence-based reasoning and clear explanation. **Do not grade private faith, sincerity or worship participation.** A proposed exercise is neither an authenticated juristic ruling nor a learner result.

## Trivium in this subject

- **Grammar:** name and accurately use selected Arabic/English terms; distinguish Qur'an, hadith, historical narration, translation and scholarly commentary.
- **Logic:** test source attribution and inference, preserve unknown authenticity, and compare competing explanations fairly.
- **Rhetoric:** give a respectful, checkable account grounded in reviewed and explicitly cited sources.

## Gates before course registration

Agree the learner audience and check entry skills; select the teaching approach and qualified scholarly reviewers; verify cited texts, hadith authenticity claims and juristic differences; agree accessibility, materials and teaching time. Only then assign course and outcome IDs and author real units, lessons and assessments for review under the [curriculum model](../../../docs/curriculum-model.md). Store individual learner records privately.
