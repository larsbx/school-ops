## Proposed entry evidence

Ask the learner to demonstrate **compose shapes from parts** from a prior planning bin, using an example and explanation. Do not assume it was taught.

## Proposed sequence (not authored units)

| Order | Investigation | Observable demonstration to develop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Compare edges and corners | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 2 | Measure with repeated units | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 3 | Partition shapes | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |

The ordering and difficulty are proposals, not grade-based prerequisite claims. Real outcomes, prerequisite dependencies, tasks and success criteria must be authored after checking entry work.

## Proposed culminating performance

**Candidate task:** Draw two paths and justify their lengths.

**Assessment gap:** Specify actual tasks, administration conditions, supports, teacher key or model responses, and threshold criteria. Proposed dimensions: accurate representation and naming; justified reason or counterexample; and an explanation another learner could check. These are **not** assessment results.

## Trivium in this subject

- **Grammar:** use geometric forms and measures precisely.
- **Logic:** investigate invariants and constructions.
- **Rhetoric:** communicate a labeled diagram with reasons.
