- Course ID and outcome IDs: **unassigned**; do not reserve before scope approval

## Proposed entry evidence

Ask the learner to demonstrate **check claims against examples** from a prior planning bin, using an example and explanation. Do not assume it was taught.

## Proposed sequence (not authored units)

| Order | Investigation | Observable demonstration to develop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Trace if-then relationships | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 2 | Explain chained decisions | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 3 | Separate necessary facts | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |

The ordering and difficulty are proposals, not grade-based prerequisite claims. Real outcomes, prerequisite dependencies, tasks and success criteria must be authored after checking entry work.

## Proposed culminating performance

**Candidate task:** Defend a chain of reasoning and identify its premises.

**Assessment gap:** Specify actual tasks, administration conditions, supports, teacher key or model responses, and threshold criteria. Proposed dimensions: accurate representation and naming; justified reason or counterexample; and an explanation another learner could check. These are **not** assessment results.

Its local labels `G3R-P1`–`G3R-P3` are **not** canonical curriculum identifiers. The pilot makes the proposed inference strand inspectable; it does **not** adopt this course, replace Islamic source-literacy work, show learner mastery, or validate a calendar. Real scope, resource/access, assessment freshness, teacher suitability and final criteria remain owner-review decisions.

## Trivium in this subject

- **Grammar:** use definitions and proposition terms precisely.
- **Logic:** investigate inference, conditions and counterexamples.
- **Rhetoric:** communicate a reproducible argument.
