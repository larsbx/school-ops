# Grade 4 — Language and composition

- Record kind: **planning scaffold**, not a course, unit, lesson or assessment
- Course ID and outcome IDs: **unassigned**; do not reserve before scope approval
- Teaching status: **not ready**; no lesson or assessment is claimed as authored
- Intended audience: Grade 4 (skills must be checked)
- Primary study: Grammar and rhetoric
- Session length, frequency, dates and access: **not selected**
- Framework: Trivium and selected Quadrivium arts; music excluded

## Proposed entry evidence

Ask the learner to demonstrate **support a claim with textual detail** from a prior planning bin, using an example and explanation. Do not assume it was taught.

## Proposed sequence (not authored units)

| Order | Investigation | Observable demonstration to develop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Compare literal and figurative meaning | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 2 | Revise paragraph coherence | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |
| 3 | Compare perspectives | Show an example and representation, test a claim, and explain the reasoning with appropriate terms. |

The ordering and difficulty are proposals, not grade-based prerequisite claims. Real outcomes, prerequisite dependencies, tasks and success criteria must be authored after checking entry work.

## Proposed culminating performance

**Candidate task:** Compare two narrators using precise evidence.

**Assessment gap:** Specify actual tasks, administration conditions, supports, teacher key or model responses, and threshold criteria. Proposed dimensions: accurate representation and naming; justified reason or counterexample; and an explanation another learner could check. These are **not** assessment results.

## Trivium in this subject

- **Grammar:** use sentences, vocabulary and source terminology precisely.
- **Logic:** investigate interpretation and textual evidence.
- **Rhetoric:** communicate clear speaking and writing.

## Authored perspectives and revision pilot (unregistered)

The [Grade 4 original perspectives pilot](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/README.md) adds a **draft, not ready** four-episode Trivium sequence with an [invented three-card source packet](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/source-packet.md), four [entry checks](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/entry-learner.md) and [remediation/rechecks](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/entry-teacher.md), eight [guided learner tasks](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/guided-learner.md) with a [complete teacher key](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/guided-teacher.md), plus ten **publicly exposed** [garden reference tasks](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/draft-assessments/reference-learner.md) and [keyed outcome-by-outcome evidence decisions](../../language/grade-4-perspectives-pilot/draft-assessments/reference-teacher.md). Local P1–P4 goals are not stable IDs. This progresses beyond the [Grade 3 language work](../../language/grade-3-evidence-writing-pilot/README.md) by comparing partial narrators, interpreting figurative sentences and revising claims for an absent reader. No reviewed classroom assessment, course registration, delivery, observed skill or ready status follows. The [Islamic studies source-review guide](../islamic-studies-guide.md) remains in force across all grades.
