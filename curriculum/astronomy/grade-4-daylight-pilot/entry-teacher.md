# Entry key and fresh rechecks — teacher only

Proposed entry criteria: each component of a check must be independently demonstrated; no total score compensates for missing prerequisite. Accept equivalent speech, writing or tactile placements. If incomplete, model with different numbers, fade answer cues, retain access supports and use the recheck below. Record only private evidence; no learner information in Git.

| Entry | Complete expected evidence | Targeted teaching and different recheck (with key) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | 60 min; 20 + 15 = 35 min. | Use a clock split. Recheck 11:50 to 12:25: 10 + 25 = 35 min; one hour = 60 min. |
| E2 | Tuesday; 55 − 40 = 15 min. | Align labeled duration strips. Recheck A 35, B 65, C 50 min: B is longest, 30 min longer than A. |
| E3 | Positions 2 and 5; 3 cells. | Model a different scale example. Recheck one cell per 5 min; 15 and 35 min: positions 3 and 7, 4 cells apart. |
| E4 | East then south; 11:00 is unknown. | Distinguish blank from a recorded label. Recheck 07:00 north, 09:00 blank, 11:00 east: north then east; 09:00 unknown. |

E1 gates episode 1; E2 and episode 1 evidence gate episode 2; E3 gates episode 3; E4 and episode 3 evidence gate episode 4. Revisit missing evidence rather than labeling a learner deficient. Entry check measures starting skills only.
