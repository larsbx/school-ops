# Guided practice — learner

Use the [fictional packet](records.md). Explain each result with a unit, label or evidence reference. These are coached examples, not assessment of mastery.

**G1.** Find Alder April 15 daylight duration by splitting at noon; express in hours/minutes and total minutes.

**G2.** Find Alder October 15 duration. Someone says it is 10 h because 17 − 7 = 10. Explain the missing minutes.

**G3.** Find all four complete Alder durations in minutes. Draw a labeled common-scale bar chart (one 100-minute cell; mark partial cells) or give a table of bar lengths in cells. Mark November as unknown. Which complete date is longest and by how many minutes compared with January?

**G4.** Can the Birch July value replace Alder November to show a seasonal trend at Alder? Name two reasons.

**G5.** On the 15-degree strip mark 105, 180 and 255 degrees. Give positions and clockwise change from first to last.

**G6.** A marker turns clockwise from 330 to 30 degrees. Show how many degrees and cells it turns, and why subtracting 330 from 30 alone is misleading.

**G7.** Compare apparent bearing changes for 08:00→10:00 and 10:00→12:00. Are changes equal in those equal time intervals?

**G8.** A report reads “The record proves the Sun was at 225 degrees at 14:00 and tells us why it moves.” Rewrite the report using two recorded sampled values and both limits (the unsampled bearing and the cause).
