# Entry prompts — daylight and direction

Draft diagnostic; no grade, prior enrollment or completed work is assumed. Use a blank clock or number line, read aloud if needed, and respond by speaking, writing or moving labeled markers. No solved examples during the check. Allow time without a speed score.

**E1.** How many minutes are in one hour? How many minutes from 09:40 to 10:15? Show a split at 10:00.

**E2.** Three labeled durations are Monday 40 min, Tuesday 55 min, Wednesday 45 min. Which is longest and by how much compared with Monday?

**E3.** On a strip with one cell per 10 minutes, place markers at 20 and 50 minutes. Give the number of cells between them.

**E4.** A record says 09:00: east, 11:00: blank, 13:00: south. Name the two recorded directions in order. Can you identify the 11:00 direction from this record?
