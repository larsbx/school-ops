# Grade 3 astronomy entry E1–E4 — learner (draft)

These are original neutral **entry checks**, not an assessment of a child's prior schooling. No outdoor observations are required. Use oral, scribed, raised-line, enlarged or AAC responses. The teacher decides access supports in private; do not put names or scores in Git.

**E1 — Draw and read a scale.** On a number line labelled 0, 10, 20, 30 (equally spaced), indicate where 15 belongs. Describe why 15 belongs halfway between 10 and 20 rather than between 20 and 30.

**E2 — Read clock intervals.** A paper record has one entry at 09:00, one at 12:00, and one at 15:00 on the **same day**. Put them in order and report the hours between first and second, and between second and third. These are labels of a fictional log, not assigned observation times.

**E3 — Measure and compare.** One drawn line is exactly 8 cm and a second exactly 13 cm on a supplied calibrated ruler. Which is longer, and by how many centimetres? Explain how the endpoints and zero of the ruler matter.

**E4 — Claim versus evidence.** A fictional chart records “stake shadow at 09:00: 24 cm” and “stake shadow at 12:00: 11 cm.” Classify: (a) “The recorded 12:00 value is smaller” and (b) “12:00 is the shortest shadow time of the entire day.” Which follows from these two entries, and what is missing for the other?
