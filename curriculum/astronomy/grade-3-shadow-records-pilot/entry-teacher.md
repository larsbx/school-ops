# Entry E1–E4 — teacher key and conditional routes (draft, not ready)

Keep separate from [learner prompts](entry-learner.md). Each gate is independent and can be administered in an accessible mode; the numerical or inferential criterion remains unchanged. Do not assign a learner diagnosis or infer mastery. Source: original fictional prompts.

| Gate | Exact model answer / proposed check | If evidence is absent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | 15 is 5 above 10 and 5 below 20; equidistant midpoint of the equally spaced 10–20 segment; accept tactile or spoken interval reasoning. | Build equal-spaced markers and place 12 or 18 correctly on a fresh line; check again before Lesson 3. |
| E2 | 09:00, 12:00, 15:00; **3 hours** then **3 hours** on the same labelled day. | Move cards on a 24-hour paper clock; independently compare 08:00 and 11:00 before Lessons 2–3. |
| E3 | 13 cm is **5 cm** longer than 8 cm. Both segments start at zero (or subtract a nonzero starting reading). | Align a ruler and count intervals, not labels; test new 7 cm and 12 cm segments before Lessons 1–2 and 4. |
| E4 | (a) is a report-supported comparison: **11 < 24**. (b) is not supported because unrecorded times may have shorter shadows. | Sort neutral “recorded / inferred / unknown” examples; recheck with new data before Lessons 1 and 4. |

**Proposed success rule:** E1–E4 each require a correct answer **and relevant explanation**; a composite grade is invalid. Failed gate => give targeted support, ask a new unmemorized example, log the result privately, and revise pacing. Vision, language, coordination and communication format alone are not mathematics/science errors. The evidence in these fixed prompts cannot prove actual outdoor observing ability or astronomical understanding.
