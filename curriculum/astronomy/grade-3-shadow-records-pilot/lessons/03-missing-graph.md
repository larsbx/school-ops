# Lesson 3 — a graph must preserve absent readings (DRAFT; not ready)

**Local targets:** G3-AST-P2 and P4. **Entry:** E1/E2 (plus refer back to E3 for measuring). **Materials proposed:** large numbered grid or raised axes, accessible point labels, original fictional data. **25-minute example**, not an adopted schedule. [Key](../guided-teacher.md).

| Trivium phase | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| Grammar: horizontal clock axis, vertical length axis, scale, missing | 5 |
| Logic: distinguish zero from unknown and extrapolation | 7 |
| Guided: draw G4 and critique G5 | 6 |
| Rhetoric: verbal graph description for a partner | 4 |
| Exit X3: correctly plot and preserve gap | 3 |

**Original practice rows:** day A = (09:00, 32 cm), (12:00, 12 cm), (15:00, 27 cm); day B = (09:00, 30 cm), (12:00, **not recorded**), (15:00, 25 cm). Set horizontal ticks 09:00/12:00/15:00 equally spaced at three-hour increments. Vertical y-axis from 0 to 40 cm in equal 10 cm increments. Plot A as points. For B, no dot at 12:00. Do not draw a line through the gap and then present its crossing value as measured evidence.

**Learner:** complete [G4/G5](../guided-learner.md). Ask “Would a blank entry mean a 0 cm shadow? Would connecting dots make a missing entry real?” Require a different justification for each.

**X3 — independent response:** Graph the three day-A points on the defined axes and show the day-B noon entry as unknown, not 0. Give one sentence specifying why the missing entry cannot be compared. Check point coordinates, axis units, scale and missing-value handling separately. For vision-independent modes, a tactile three-column board with 0/10/20/30/40 rows is equivalent evidence.
