## Arithmetic consistency ledger (for test replay)

- 14 is the **day A** lowest of (34, 14, 28); 34−14=20.
- At 08:30 A−B=34−33=1; B's 11:30 value is **unknown**, not zero.
- For day C the recorded values (36, 15, 30) give 15 < 30 < 36.
- 08:30 to 11:30 to 14:30 comprises **3-hour** increments.
- Graph ticks: 0, 10, 20, 30, 40 cm; other equally spaced sequence is invalid as labelled.
