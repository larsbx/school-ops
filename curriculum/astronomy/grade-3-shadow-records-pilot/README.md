**Status: DRAFT, not ready.** Authored, unregistered, original **shadow-record literacy pilot** for proposed Grade 3; not an approved course, unit, lesson, timetable, real observation, secure test or claim of learner mastery. Formal course, unit, lesson, outcome, assessment and resource identifiers are **unassigned** until scope approval. G3-AST-P1–P4 are disposable local authoring labels. Prepared 2026-11-12.

## Boundary and provenance

Primary selected Quadrivium art: **astronomy** (comparing appearances of sunlight-derived shadows at recorded times); geometry measures segments; arithmetic compares measurements and intervals. **Music excluded.** Grammar names observation, timestamp, length, units, missing entry and hypothesis; logic examines measurement comparability and unsupported inference; rhetoric presents a reproducible limited claim. The numerical records here are **fictional constructed data**, not measurements of sunlight, sunrise, location, seasons, solar noon or lunar cycles. Their arithmetic is checkable; they cannot establish a physical astronomical phenomenon. No externally authored images, reports or passages are reproduced.

This is **not** the Grade 2 mathematics astronomy Unit 5 in [open PR #41](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/pull/41), which addresses dated sky records/calendar intervals and lunar-cycle inference under C-MATH-001; it does not assume that draft was taught. This pilot adds fixed-stake measurement controls, graph scales, missing measurements, and comparability of observations. It also does not replace the [Grade 3 geometry proposal](../../scaffolds/grade-3/geometry.md), or the Grade 3 [arithmetic bridge](../../mathematics/grade-3-bridge-pilot/README.md). The [Grade 2 mathematics course](../../mathematics/grade-2/README.md) retains authority.

## Observation safety, access and source custody

**Do not look directly at the Sun**, use telescopes, lenses or binoculars to find it, or require unsupervised outdoor/nightside travel. If approved after review, a teacher alone establishes a level surface, fixed vertical object, identical measurement origin and safe viewing of the **ground shadow** only; no individual addresses, coordinates, timestamps linked to identifiable learners or photos are committed here. For learners unable to observe outdoors, a review-approved teacher-collected **real** de-identified record or purely **fictional** record-literacy mode must be distinct; only the former, after provenance checking, could support an actual-observation outcome. Mobility, sight, writing and language are not proxy tests for astronomical understanding: allow raised-line graphs, measuring strip/tactile edges, enlarged charts, speech, AAC or scribing without revealing a correct numerical answer. Weather, location, surface angle, object height, local clock and conditions are real confounders; a short set of sampled clock times is **not** proof of solar noon, sunrise direction, causation, exact shortest shadow or seasonal trend.
