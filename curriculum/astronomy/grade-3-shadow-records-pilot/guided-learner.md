# Shadow records — guided learner sheet G1–G8 (DRAFT)

**Original constructed examples only.** The readings are **not real sky or Sun observations**. Treat all times as hypothetical *local clock labels on one date* unless a task states otherwise. Answers and session notes belong in separate teacher materials. Use drawing, oral explanation, assistive communication, scribe, large chart or tactile axis as preferred.

**G1. A repeatable method.** A teacher wants to compare a fixed stake's shadow on a level surface. List the measurement's starting point, ending point, unit and two conditions that should stay fixed.

**G2. Changing object height.** Chart 1 shows a 20 cm tall stake and a 17 cm shadow; chart 2 shows a 40 cm tall stake and a 24 cm shadow, both labelled 10:00. Is this a controlled comparison of how time affects shadow length? Give a reason.

**G3. Three readings.** A fictional 20 cm stake has 09:00 → 32 cm, 12:00 → 12 cm, 15:00 → 27 cm. Sort the values largest to smallest and calculate (a) the difference between 09:00 and 12:00, (b) the difference between 12:00 and 15:00. Name the smallest **sampled** value, not the shortest over the day.

**G4. Make a point graph.** Plot (09:00, 32 cm), (12:00, 12 cm), (15:00, 27 cm). Horizontal ticks: 09:00, 12:00, 15:00, equally separated by **three hours**. Vertical ticks: 0, 10, 20, 30, 40 **cm**, equally separated. Explain which units belong to which axis.

**G5. Missing at 12:00.** Day B has 09:00 → 30 cm; 12:00 → **not recorded**; 15:00 → 25 cm. A friend adds (12:00, 0 cm) to fill the chart. Is this an observation, a legitimate inference, or a false entry? What should the chart show instead?

**G6. Same time, different apparatus.** One 20 cm stake gives 18 cm at 10:00. A second 40 cm stake gives 22 cm at 10:00. A student concludes that the Sun's position changed because the shadows differ. Identify a confounder and one improved comparison setup. Do not assert a solar position.

**G7. Sampled versus universal.** G3 has its smallest recorded value at 12:00. Complete: “Of the **three** measurements, ___.” Can that justify “The shortest shadow of the entire day was at 12:00”? Give a reason.

**G8. Compare matched readings.** Day A: 09:00 → 32 cm, 12:00 → 12 cm. Day B (same stated equipment): 09:00 → 30 cm, 12:00 → 11 cm. How much lower is B at each stated time? Defend a true claim about these **four entries** and name one claim the entries cannot establish.

**Safety:** No direct Sun viewing, optical equipment or unaccompanied outdoor work is needed for any question. Do not share identifying place/time records publicly.
