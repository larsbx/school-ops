## X1–X4 independent lesson-exit keys

- **X1:** M1 is a model assumption, S/A a synthetic notebook entry; neither is actual field observation.
- **X2:** plant→beetle and beetle→bird are the only supplied direct arrows; direct plant→bird does not follow from a path.
- **X3:** A→B is **2 fewer sightings**; A=5 minutes, D=10 minutes, so raw counts cannot prove population changes.
- **X4:** S/A,B,C list **6, 4, 5 beetle sightings** in 5-minute fictional rounds; values differed but population causes remain unknown. Repeat comparable timed observations and record visibility.

## Proposed noncompensatory criteria and feedback

- **P1:** R1 all three source types plus **none real** and R3/R7 labels the unsupported inference.
- **P2:** R2 both arrows and correct food-to-eater convention **and** R3 no direct shortcut.
- **P3:** R4 **2 sightings** with caveat; R5 **15** not unique; R6 **5 vs 10 minutes**; R7 two possible causes and no proof; R8 **3 sightings** and no cause.
- **P4:** R9 matched repeated design plus a residual limitation, and R10 cites S, uses a correct count and identifies uncertainty.

No compensating total or numerical percentage can hide a missing component. Give specific corrections then recheck with a **new unseen form**; the publicly exposed fixed items are unsuitable as a secure assessment.
