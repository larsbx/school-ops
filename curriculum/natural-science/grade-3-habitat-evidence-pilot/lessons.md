# Four Grade 3 habitat-evidence instructional episodes (authoring draft)

All **four 25-minute plans are proposed**, not an accepted class schedule. The [original synthetic packet](source-packet.md) is **not real field evidence**; only paper/tactile cards are required. Every episode contains explicit **Grammar, Logic and Rhetoric** with a reasoned learner action; exit keys X1–X4 are in the separate [teacher pack](teacher.md). [Learner G1–G8](learner.md) provides rehearsal. Read-aloud, tactile/large print, pointing, dictation and AAC may be used; preserve the inferential task.

## Episode 1 — notebook, premise or unsupported story (P1)

**Prerequisites:** E1/E4 or targeted reteaching. **Grammar:** observation entry versus invented model premise, sighting, prediction, causation. **Logic:** sorting and counterexample to "6 sightings = 6 real living beetles." **Rhetoric:** teach a peer how to cite a model or notebook without asserting real fieldwork.

| Minutes | Learner action | Evidence |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4 | Recheck E1 with a neutral new dots example. | Record/inference separated. |
| 6 | Sort M1, S/A and "North has more animals because of food" into premise, invented record and unsupported claim. | All three kinds distinguished. |
| 7 | Teacher models qualification; pair presents a correction with source label. | Claim tied to M/S/T. |
| 5 | Complete G1 and independently classify a new invented count card. | Explicit **not field data**. |
| 3 | **Exit X1:** Say what M1 and S/A are and whether either is a real observation. | Both types accurate and neither real. |

**Feedback:** if a learner confuses fiction with field results, contrast what a story *stipulates* with independent evidence and recheck on a different paper-only model.

## Episode 2 — food-to-eater arrows (P2)

**Prerequisites:** E3 and Episode 1, otherwise remediate. **Grammar:** direct arrow, food, eater, path. **Logic:** M1/M2 justify two direct edges; a length-two route does not license a direct shortcut. **Rhetoric:** explain arrow direction and a rejected claim to a partner using tactile arrows if needed.

| Minutes | Learner action | Evidence |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4 | Revisit E3 bread→bird. | Eater and food labeled. |
| 7 | Arrange plant/beetle/bird cards; reverse one arrow to test the convention. | Both direct arrows correct. |
| 6 | Challenge "plant→bird is a direct arrow" and use M4. | Path distinct from direct edge. |
| 5 | Complete G2–G3 and explain direction. | Source M1–M4 cited. |
| 3 | **Exit X2:** State two supplied direct arrows and one unsupported shortcut. | Two correct, shortcut rejected. |

**Feedback:** avoid turning stipulated model into a universal ecology rule. Ask which source added any proposed third arrow.

## Episode 3 — sighting sums and fair looking time (P3)

**Prerequisites:** E2 and Episode 1; provide counting manipulatives as needed. **Grammar:** sightings versus individuals, round, minute, difference, total. **Logic:** counts may double-count; different effort (5 vs 10 minutes) blocks simple inference. **Rhetoric:** state a calculation with units and an uncertainty. **Quadrivium:** genuine arithmetic (6−4 and 6+4+5), *not* astronomy or geometry.

| Minutes | Learner action | Evidence |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4 | Count and label E2-like counters. | 4+3=7 *sightings*. |
| 7 | Compute S/A−B and S/A+B+C with tiles. | 2 fewer, 15 total sightings. |
| 6 | Compare looking-time strips A versus D. | 5 vs 10 minutes, population unknown. |
| 5 | Complete G4–G6 with reasons. | Arithmetic and explicit limitation. |
| 3 | **Exit X3:** Report A→B difference and why A/D cannot establish a population trend. | Both facts and caveat. |

**Feedback:** a raw sum is not a count of distinct beetles. Do not require multiplication/division or a rate calculation.

## Episode 4 — revise a habitat claim (P4)

**Prerequisites:** Episodes 1–3, E4 access route; reteach any weak component first. **Grammar:** alternative explanation, controlled effort, detection, confounder. **Logic:** hidden animals versus fewer animals both fit counts; matching time reduces but does not eliminate ambiguity. **Rhetoric:** defend a limited explanation with a cited number and next measurement.

| Minutes | Learner action | Evidence |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4 | Recall S and T tables, naming fictional provenance. | M/S/T not confused. |
| 6 | Explain two different worlds matching A=6, B=4. | Neither proven. |
| 7 | Construct matched repeat plan for North/South with same five-minute duration, observer and conditions recorded. | Fairer design, residual uncertainty. |
| 5 | Complete G7–G8; critique partner's causal overclaim. | Better warranted statement. |
| 3 | **Exit X4:** Cite A/B/C counts, limitation and one new check. | 6/4/5; population not proved; matched repeat. |

**Feedback:** let pupils revise "proved population decline" to "fewer sightings were recorded"; if the learner cannot supply a second plausible explanation, model detection limitations then recheck with independent examples. All actual learner evidence remains private.
