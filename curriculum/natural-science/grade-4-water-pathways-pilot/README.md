# Grade 4 natural science — controlled water-pathway evidence

**Authored draft / unregistered / not ready.** This bounded pilot advances the [Grade 4 science planning scaffold](../../scaffolds/grade-4/natural-science.md): its water-pathway strand, not the separate particle-model or energy-transfer strands. There is no approved course, unit, lesson or assessment. Canonical IDs remain **unassigned** until scope approval. G4-SCI-P1–P4, E1–E4, G1–G8, X1–X4 and S1–S10 are purely local authoring labels. Four illustrative 25-minute episodes are **not** an adopted time budget.

Unlike [open Grade 3 habitat PR #55](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/pull/55), this pilot analyzes *input/output ledgers, fair comparisons, absent measurements, and bounded scientific explanation*, not food webs or actual field observations. All numbers in [Packet A and transfer Packet T](source-packet.md) are **invented for teaching**; no simulated observation is scientific evidence. The only positive mathematical art practiced is **arithmetic**: units, subtraction, two-trial averages and comparisons. The Trivium's grammar, logic and rhetoric are explicit in each episode. Setup labels 'level'/'angled' are not a formal geometry lesson; no astronomy is claimed. Music is excluded.

## Proposed observable outcomes and noncompensatory evidence

| Local proposed outcome | Given the supplied source conditions, the learner can... | Distinct independent evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G4-SCI-P1 | Read mL units, compute input minus collector volume, distinguish a reading from an unmeasured destination | E1/E2, X1, S1/S2 |
| G4-SCI-P2 | Compute replicate means/differences and identify one recorded factor changed with other recorded labels fixed | E3, X2/X3, S3–S6 |
| G4-SCI-P3 | Reject unsupported absorption/evaporation claims or confounded comparisons, naming the missing evidence | E2/E4, X3/X4, S7/S8 |
| G4-SCI-P4 | Communicate a numerically attributed, qualified conclusion and a further independently measured quantity | E4, X4, S9/S10 |

P1–P4 require evidence **separately**; correct arithmetic does not compensate for unsupported causation, and elegant prose cannot compensate for bad figures. All fixed S tasks are **public exposed reference exemplars**, not unseen mastery assessment items.

**Source boundary:** input − collector output is called **not in collector**, not 'absorbed'. The simulation does not measure water stored on a tray, absorbed, evaporated, spilled or lost by a device. Labels held constant within the *invented record* do not guarantee real experimental control.

**Access options, pending qualified review:** large-print/structured text and read-aloud of table headings and units, tactile labeled numerical cards, speech/sign/typing or exact dictation to a scribe; no mandatory liquid handling. Neutral presentation supports may continue for independent checks, but prompts revealing the correct comparison or explanation require a fresh independent form. Keep learner work, accommodations, names and individual scores outside this repository.
