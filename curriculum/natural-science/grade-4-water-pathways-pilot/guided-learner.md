# Eight guided learner tasks — fictional record literacy

**Public draft practice, not a secure assessment.** Use [Packet A](source-packet.md); no actual experiment, liquids or external facts. Include units and identify whether an answer is a record, arithmetic or inference.

**G1.** A trial 1: give input, collector amount, and computed 'not in collector'. Does the table measure absorption?

**G2.** B trial 2: compute input minus collected. Name one destination not independently measured.

**G3.** Find A and B's two-trial means and their difference.

**G4.** Find C's mean and compare it to A. What recorded label changed?

**G5.** Does A/B isolate a surface-label comparison in this ledger? Name the two matching labels.

**G6.** Someone uses B/C to claim cloth's effect. What two labels changed, and what better pair exists?

**G7.** Rewrite 'All 59 mL went into the cloth' based only on B trial 1.

**G8.** Give a two-sentence memo comparing A/B: numbers, shared conditions, and a source limitation.
