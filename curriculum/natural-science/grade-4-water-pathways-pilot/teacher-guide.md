## Access and safety

Provide named-column linear versions of tables, large print, screen-reader-ready units, tactile labeled integer cards, neutral read-aloud, speech/sign/typing or exact scribing. A calculator may support number operations when approved, but a teacher must separately assess a learner's reasoned claim. Do not require fine-motor handling of water, home observations or private details. Any *real* apparatus would require source reliability, adult supervision, calibration, spill/sanitation risk, accessible alternative and local consent review; none is assumed. Individual scoring, accommodations or actual learner results belong in private records only.

## Candidate noncompensatory scoring and review blockers

P1: correct mL, difference **and** measured/computed distinction. P2: accurate replicate means/gaps **and** label-controlled comparisons. P3: a missing-measure argument and rejection of unjustified cause. P4: an attributed bounded memo and additional measurable test. A partial success calls for targeted reteaching, not an invented mastery score; fixed public reference items cannot demonstrate secure transfer.

Owner and qualified subject reviewers must approve intended grade scope, epistemic language, math and source accuracy, materials safety, time, task ambiguity, accessibility parity, thresholds, fresh alternate forms, copyright/permissions and canonical record IDs.
