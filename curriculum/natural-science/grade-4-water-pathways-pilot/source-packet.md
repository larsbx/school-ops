# Packet A and Packet T — invented training records

**Entirely fictional numerical simulation**: not measurements from a classroom or validated facts about actual flow, erosion, water absorption or evaporation. Treat a reported collector number as a datum *inside the fiction*, not scientific verification. These data are original and contain no personal records or third-party materials.

## Claim/source ladder

- **Reported in fictional ledger:** collector value, input volume and labeled setup.
- **Calculated from the ledger:** difference, mean or input-minus-collector amount.
- **Not established:** whether the remaining volume soaked, evaporated, spilled, pooled, or was lost due to an apparatus; hidden real-world controls; any causal law.
- A genuine future observation would need reviewed methods, calibrated collection and measurements of other possible destinations, with safe accessible handling.
