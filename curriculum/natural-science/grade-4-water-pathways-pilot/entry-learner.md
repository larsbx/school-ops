# Four independent proposed entry prompts — learner

**Public draft only.** No real liquid is required; respond in speech, writing, typed text, accessible number cards or exact scribing. Rechecks use different data.

**E1.** An invented trial begins with 70 mL and a collector shows 46 mL. Compute the amount not in the collector and name its unit.

**E2.** 'The marked cup reads 24 mL; therefore 6 mL evaporated.' Name the *reported reading* and explain whether evaporation was independently measured.

**E3.** Two trays each have 80 mL input, identical tilt and different surface labels. A third has 100 mL input and different tilt. Which two isolate a difference in recorded surface label, and why?

**E4.** Invented collectors show 50 and 38 mL after equal inputs. Write one numerical comparison and one limitation on what is known.
