# Complete guided teacher key

**Teacher-only draft key**, for [Packet A](source-packet.md). Guide learners to distinguish reading, arithmetic and mechanism; do not use answer cues during independent checks.

| Task | Model answer | Feedback if wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | **100 mL** input, **72 mL** collector, **100−72=28 mL** not collected; absorption **not measured** | A computed remainder is not a destination |
| G2 | **100−39=61 mL** not in collector; evaporation, pooling, spilling and absorption are all unmeasured | Do not choose a mechanism without evidence |
| G3 | A **(72+68)/2=70 mL**; B **(41+39)/2=40 mL**; gap **30 mL** | A mean uses both trials, not only one |
| G4 | C **(26+30)/2=28 mL** versus A **70 mL**; gap **42 mL**; tilt label differs | Do not call a simulated gap a real effect |
| G5 | Yes in recorded labels: same **100 mL input**, both **angled**; surface is bare/cloth | Hidden controls are not guaranteed in real life |
| G6 | B angled/cloth, C level/bare: **tilt and surface** differ; use **A/B** for surface | A confounded contrast cannot isolate one label |
| G7 | 'B trial 1 shows **41 mL collected** from **100 mL** input, leaving **59 mL not in collector**; destination unknown.' | Measured absorption is a different variable |
| G8 | 'In the *invented ledger*, A's mean is **70 mL** and B's **40 mL**, a **30 mL difference**, with both inputs 100 mL and tilt angled. The packet does not measure absorption or prove a physical cause.' | Attribute, compute, control and qualify |
