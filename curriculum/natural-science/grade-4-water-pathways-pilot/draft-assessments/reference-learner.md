# Exposed reference transfer tasks — learner

**Draft public exemplars, not unseen or secure assessment.** Read invented [Packet T](../source-packet.md). Numbers are not actual empirical evidence. Include mL and qualify claims about water destinations. Accepted access modes are described in the pilot's teacher protocol.

**S1.** D trial 1: state recorded input and collector readings, compute the amount not in collector, and say whether this is measured absorption.

**S2.** E trial 2: calculate input minus collector with units.

**S3.** Compute D and E's two-trial collector means and their difference.

**S4.** Compute F's collector mean and compare with D's.

**S5.** Identify a pair that differs in surface label only among recorded factors. State the matching tilt and input.

**S6.** Can E/F isolate surface effects? Name any additional recorded factor that differs.

**S7.** Assess: 'E trial 1's cloth absorbed exactly 44 mL.' Give the computed quantity and explain the missing observation.

**S8.** Calculate D and G means; decide if their raw 30 mL difference demonstrates a stronger physical flow effect. Include input volumes.

**S9.** Write a bounded two-sentence memo using D/E with averaged figures, controls and one unknown.

**S10.** Propose a new kind of measurement that would help distinguish destinations of uncollected water, while stating at least two setup labels to hold fixed. Explain why Packet T cannot answer the question.
