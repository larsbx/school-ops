# Grade 4 mathematics: same-whole fractions and common-unit sums

**Authored proposal, draft / not ready.** This is an unregistered Grade 4 **pilot**, not a course, ready lesson, assessment, timetable, learner record or achievement claim. Formal C/U/L/O/A/R identifiers remain **unassigned** until owner scope approval. Local P1–P4 and E/G/X/S item labels are only authoring labels. Four illustrative 25-minute episodes are not an adopted schedule. There are no external sources, licensed extracts, real student data or invented observations in this package.

This develops the [Grade 4 mathematics planning bin](../../scaffolds/grade-4/mathematics.md) rather than repeating [Grade 2 mathematics](../grade-2/README.md) or the [Grade 3 arithmetic bridge](../grade-3-bridge-pilot/README.md). Grade 3 explores equal parts; Grade 4 now investigates the **invariant whole when parts are renamed**, **common part-units for sums and differences**, and **false inferences caused by mixing wholes**. The Grade 3 geometry pilot instead addresses tiling/perimeter; our rectangle strips represent fractional arithmetic, not a claim to complete independent geometry.

## Proposed outcomes and evidence

| Local goal | Observable skill | Fixed exposed reference evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Name numerator, denominator and the fixed whole; distinguish relative fraction from physical amount | S1, S2, S9: correct units and a valid same-whole reason |
| P2 | Partition an unchanged whole into finer equal units, justify `1/2=2/4=4/8` and `2/3=4/6` | S3, S4, S10: correct equivalence with conserved shade |
| P3 | Add/subtract using a shared sixth, fourth, eighth or tenth, including a full whole | S5–S8, S10: correct unit model, numerical answer and reason |
| P4 | Refute wrong denominator arithmetic or invalid comparisons of different-sized wholes | S2, S6, S7, S9, S10: specific counterexample and valid repair |

**Prerequisites:** administer the [four actual entry prompts](entry-learner.md) and use [skill-specific teacher decisions/rechecks](entry-teacher.md), rather than assuming Grade 3 study was implemented or mastered. Remediate the missing skill and administer a **different** recheck, never substitute an averaged score.

**Teaching:** [four scripted Trivium episodes](lessons.md) with exit questions X1–X4, [guided learner G1–G8](guided-learner.md) and [teacher key with feedback](teacher-guide.md). **Reference:** [fixed learner form S1–S10](draft-assessments/reference-learner.md) and [complete separate teacher key/rubric](draft-assessments/reference-teacher.md). All reference tasks and keys are publicly exposed: they cannot establish unseen independent mastery.

**Trivium:** grammar names unit, equal partition, numerator and denominator; logic checks equivalence against counters/length and builds counterexamples; rhetoric explains a claim with a labeled strip and checkable reason in every lesson. **Quadrivium:** genuine fractional arithmetic; equal-length strip diagrams support its measure, but do not independently certify a geometry unit. Observational astronomy remains elsewhere in the 13×8 plan. **Music is excluded.**

**Accessibility:** propose identical-length tactile strips with detectable equal divisions, read-aloud of all prose, non-color-only filled/empty symbols, high-contrast enlarged diagrams, speech/typing/drawing/AAC response choices and adult transcription without solving. Validate that these routes preserve the same arithmetic demand. No timed score or motor/English fluency threshold. No private learner results or accommodations in Git.
