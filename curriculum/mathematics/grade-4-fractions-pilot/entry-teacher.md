# E1–E4 teacher key and distinct rechecks

**Draft / not ready.** Each row is a separate entry decision, not a compensating total. Keep this key out of the learner copy.

| Gate | Exact expected evidence | Response if missing | Fresh recheck and answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | `3/4`; four names **equal portions of one whole**, three shaded; double-width cell violates equal partition | Fold four identical cells and contrast unequal lengths | Five equal cells, two marked = **2/5**; unequal widths cannot certify fifths |
| E2 | Half 12 = **6 cm**, half 8 = **4 cm**; different wholes, so halves differ in length | Lay both against same ruler; distinguish fraction from measured size | Half 14 = **7 cm**, half 10 = **5 cm** |
| E3 | `1/2=2/4=3/6` of fixed 12 cells, all **6 cells**; refining cuts preserves shade | Re-cut physically unchanged strip into groups of 6, 3, 2 | On a 12-cell whole, shade 4; **1/3=2/6=4/12**, all 4 cells |
| E4 | False: `1/4+1/4=2/4=1/2`; `2/8=1/4` is only one of four cells | Model common-size cells, then fade worked hints | `1/8+3/8=4/8=1/2`, not `4/16` |

Require E1 and E2 before same-whole lesson, E3 before equivalence, and E4 before unsupported-rule diagnosis. Allow oral, tactile, visual or AAC proof; require mathematical accuracy and an independent reason rather than handwriting speed. Record any real response privately, not in this repo.
