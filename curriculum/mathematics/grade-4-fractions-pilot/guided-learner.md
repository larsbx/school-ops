# G1–G8 guided learner tasks

**Draft, not a secret test.** Use a single fixed whole within each prompt except where explicitly comparing two whole lengths. State the common part size and a reason. Non-written explanations are acceptable.

**G1.** A 16-cm strip is quartered. How long is one quarter? Why is “4” not a measurement in cm?

**G2.** Shade 1/4 on 12-cm and 20-cm strips. Find both actual shaded lengths; can the lengths be equal?

**G3.** Shade four of eight equal cells; show the amount in halves, fourths and eighths.

**G4.** On one 12-cm whole, are 2/3 and 4/6 equal? Explain with both lengths.

**G5.** Use four equal cells to add 1/4+1/2 and state a shared unit.

**G6.** Use eight equal cells to compute 5/8−1/4 and explain conversion.

**G7.** A pupil suggests 1/3+1/6=2/9. Diagnose the error and repair with six cells.

**G8.** A pupil says “half of a 4-cm strip is longer than one-third of a 12-cm strip, because 1/2 is bigger.” Find the lengths and correct the reasoning.
