# Entry check E1–E4 — learner

**Draft; exposed, not secure.** An equal cell is equal in length to every other cell **of the same strip**. Use tactile counters, a printed/large diagram or an equivalent spoken model. Explain by voice, drawing, writing or AAC; no time limit.

**E1.** Four equal cells, three shaded: `[X][X][X][_]`. Name the shaded fraction, what 4 describes, and why a strip with a double-width first cell cannot be called four equal parts.

**E2.** Two strips measure 12 cm and 8 cm. Find half of each in cm. Are their halves equal in length? Explain why the fraction symbol alone does not decide.

**E3.** On a fixed 12-cell strip shade the first 6 cells. Write the shade in halves, fourths and sixths. Explain why the amount is unchanged.

**E4.** A pupil says `1/4+1/4=2/8`. On a four-cell whole, shade the claimed sum and explain or correct the pupil's computation.

Use a different recheck after support. These are only proposed checks; no learner skill is presumed or recorded here.
