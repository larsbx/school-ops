**Teacher feedback protocol:** classify wrong whole vs unequal partition vs failed renaming vs arithmetic vs unsupported explanation. Model a fresh example, fade hints without removing access supports, require a different independent recheck. For P1/P2, insist on correct whole and equivalence rationale; for P3, calculation **and** common-unit representation; for P4, a falsifying example and valid repair. Avoid compensating scores.
