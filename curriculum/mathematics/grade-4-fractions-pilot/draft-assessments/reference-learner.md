# Grade 4 fractions: S1–S10 fixed reference learner form

**DRAFT / not ready / publicly exposed.** This is **not** an unseen or secure mastery assessment. Same whole for each item unless multiple whole lengths are explicitly given. Read-aloud, identical-size tactile or enlarged partitions, speech/drawing/AAC answers allowed. No time limit.

**S1.** Shade three of eight equal cells on a single strip. Name the fraction and what numerator and denominator count; why must the cells be equal?

**S2.** Half of a 12-cm strip and half of an 18-cm strip: find both lengths and refute or justify “these shaded lengths match.”

**S3.** On a single eight-cell whole shade four cells. Rename that shade in halves, fourths and eighths. What was preserved?

**S4.** On one 12-cm strip, compare 2/3 and 4/6. Calculate each length and explain equivalence.

**S5.** On a four-cell strip, add 1/2+1/4. Show the conversion and the number of shaded cells.

**S6.** On an eight-cell whole, compute 3/8+1/4. Why is 4/12 false?

**S7.** A pupil writes 3/4−1/8=2/(−4). Repair using eight equal cells and identify the wrong rule.

**S8.** On a six-cell whole, add 2/3+1/3. Is the answer less than, equal to, or more than one whole? Explain.

**S9.** Which physical shade is longer: 1/2 of 6 cm or 1/3 of 15 cm? Why cannot the symbols alone settle this comparison?

**S10.** On a ten-cell strip, correct 2/5+1/10=3/15. Show an equal-part representation and explain the false rule.

No teacher answers or learner-specific responses belong in this sheet.
