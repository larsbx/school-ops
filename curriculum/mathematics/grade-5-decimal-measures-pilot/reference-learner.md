# Reference form — draft, publicly exposed

Not a secure assessment. Proposed untimed access supports and approximately 25 minutes of work; speed is not scored. All lengths are fictional, exact unless specified. Use 1 m = 100 cm and explain your reasoning. Blank strips, blank place columns, spoken text and dictated responses are allowed; no worked examples or answer feedback during an independent attempt.

**S1.** Write 1.24 m as metres, tenths and hundredths, and then as centimetres.

**S2.** Decide whether 0.30 m and 0.3 m are equal. Show why using equal-sized units.

**S3.** Complete the comparison for row A below, showing both quantities in hundredths of a metre.

**S4.** Complete the comparison for row B below in the same way.

| Row | Fraction of one metre | Decimal metres |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 9/20 | 0.5 |
| B | 23/50 | 0.46 |

**S5.** Compare 3/5 of a 50 cm strip with 1/4 of a 100 cm strip. Identify the different wholes and state which is longer.

**S6.** Use budget row C below to calculate the remainder and check it.

**S7.** Does row D meet the minimum remainder? Explain the boundary.

**S8.** Does row E meet the minimum remainder? If not, how many more centimetres of stock are required? Explain.

| Row | Available (m) | First use (m) | Second use (m) | Minimum left (cm) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C | 1.80 | 0.72 | 3/4 | 0 |
| D | 1.50 | 0.65 | 3/5 | 25 |
| E | 1.20 | 0.54 | 1/2 | 20 |

**S9.** Correct the claim: “0.07 m is greater than 0.6 m because 7 is greater than 6.” Explain why the place units matter.

**S10.** Two fictional plans each need a 0.85 m piece and a 0.45 m piece. Stock A is 1.30 m and stock B is 1.35 m. Compare the plans. A real cutting allowance is unrecorded. Recommend stock for the exact model and explain what additional information is needed before promising real feasibility.
