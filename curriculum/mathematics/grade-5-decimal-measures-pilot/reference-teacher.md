## Provisional criterion and response

For each task record three separate observations: representation/units correct; calculation or comparison correct; explanation/condition correct. Missing a required observation is not a complete task. Proposed noncompensatory evidence rule: all listed tasks for each P outcome complete on a fresh reviewed form; a high total cannot replace a missing outcome. This conservative draft threshold is subject to owner review, not an adopted grade expectation.

If an explanation is absent but arithmetic correct, ask for a reason on a fresh equivalent prompt rather than supplying one. Check P1 before P2 and P3; use the entry route and reteach the specific misconception. P4 requires a justified conditional argument, not merely a choice. Review omission controls (wrong whole, wrong place value, wrong minimum inequality, unrecorded allowance) before approving parallel forms. Keep individual responses, accommodations and judgments in the private system only.
