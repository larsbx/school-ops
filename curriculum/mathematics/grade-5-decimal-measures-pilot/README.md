# Grade 5 mathematics — decimal place, fractional measures and exact decisions

**Draft, unregistered, not ready.** Intended audience Grade 5; all entry skills must be checked. Local labels P1–P4, E/R/G/X/S are only task cross-references; canonical identifiers are unassigned. Four proposed 25-minute episodes plus separate entry and 25-minute reference check; not an adopted timetable.

## Scope and progression

Extend the Grade 4 planning work on equivalent fractions and common units into decimal notation and exact comparisons of lengths. The Grade 4 fraction draft is not assumed taught or approved. This is not a repeat of Grade 2 place-value drills: learners justify equivalence across fractions, decimals and centimetres, including a common-whole restriction, and use the results in a two-stage decision. Exclude repeating decimals, rounding, area conversion and general fraction division from this pilot. All workshop lengths and plans are original fictional records, not measured or guaranteed manufacturing tolerances.

| Local outcome | Observable performance | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Express a length up to 3 m in metres to hundredths and in whole centimetres; justify trailing-zero equality by regrouping | G1–G2, X1, S1–S2 |
| P2 | Convert proper fractions of the same metre with denominators 2, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100 to hundredths and compare exactly; identify when wholes differ | G3–G4, X2, S3–S5 |
| P3 | Compute a two-step length budget using a single unit and test feasibility, including an exact boundary | G5–G6, X3, S6–S8 |
| P4 | Correct a false comparison or recommendation and communicate a decision supported by stated assumptions and a checkable calculation | G7–G8, X4, S9–S10 |

## Materials and access

Use metre strips (or tactile labelled strips), blank 10-by-10 hundredths grids representing one metre by 100 equal **length shares** (not square centimetres), centimetre number lines, place-value columns, counters, pencil and paper. A linear strip may replace a grid. Supply a large-print or spoken table and allow dictation, pointing, movable digits or accessible keyboard entry. Do not give the conversion or comparison result. Reading and motor speed are not criteria. A calculator is not needed; if selected as an accommodation, still require a model or exact regrouping and verify access equivalence before adoption.
