# Entry and later recheck — draft learner sheet

Do the E prompts before the lesson, using a drawing, objects or an oral explanation. The teacher will offer the R prompts after any needed teaching, not as hints during the first attempt. These are practice checks, not grades.

**E1.** One metre is 100 centimetres. Show 47 cm as four tens and seven ones of centimetres. How many more centimetres make one metre?

**E2.** A strip is cut into four equal parts. Mark three parts. Explain why three quarters of this strip is the same length as six eighths of the same strip.

**E3.** A tray has room for 95 cm of strips. Two strips use 38 cm and 27 cm. How much space remains? Show two steps.

**E4.** A 60 cm strip and an 80 cm strip are each cut in half. Are the halves equal lengths? Explain using numbers.

## Separate rechecks

**R1.** Show 63 cm as tens and ones of centimetres. How many more make 100 cm?

**R2.** Mark two fifths of a strip. Explain why four tenths of the same strip is the same length.

**R3.** There is room for 90 cm. Two pieces use 24 cm and 39 cm. How much remains? Show two steps.

**R4.** Compare half of a 50 cm strip with half of a 70 cm strip. Explain.
