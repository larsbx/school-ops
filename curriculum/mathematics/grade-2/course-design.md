This is one selectable implementation of the\n[instructional pattern library](../../../templates/instruction/README.md).\nIts sixteen modules are an illustrative publishing layout; other lessons can use\nKhan-style practice, test–teach–test, fading, retrieval or inquiry as appropriate.\nThe existing seven curriculum units and outcome definitions remain authoritative.\n\n## The learner experience

Begin with a concrete situation worth explaining. Show a short mathematical
exchange, investigate the relationship with objects and notation, practice it,
then communicate enough information for another person to reconstruct or test it.
Return to the relationship in independent work and a fresh application.
Every module has two cycles with distinct goals; a cycle may span sessions.
Their order and activity mix can vary when prerequisites remain satisfied.

| Structural feature | Original mathematics implementation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Context opener | Objects, a diagram, a small table or a practical problem | Learner identifies the quantity, counted unit or question |
| Modeled exchange | Teacher and partner demonstrate a claim and ask why | Learner connects words, representation and reason |
| Language and notation | Name units, read symbols, interpret equality and labels | Accurate names and meanings, with reading/spelling support |
| Mathematical focus | Investigate a numerical or geometric relationship | Model, invariant, comparison, inverse check or counterexample |
| Guided work | Solve a few bounded examples with feedback | Record the support used; revise a mistaken representation |
| Listen and reconstruct | Build a model from a short spoken description | Reconstructed quantities agree with the description |
| Read, draw and write | Interpret a model or short story; record a result and reason | Mathematical content is correct even when an adult scribes |
| Partner exchange | Each partner has information the other needs | Recipient reconstructs the intended quantity or tests the claim |
| Independent practice | Workbook reinforces exactly the taught cycle | Accurate work and an explanation where requested |
| Review and application | Fresh keyed tasks plus a learner reflection | Outcome-specific evidence; reflection alone does not establish mastery |

Grammar supplies names, units and notation; logic supplies the conservation,
inference or counterexample; rhetoric makes the explanation inspectable by a
partner. Mathematical focus is not merely a new heading for rote procedures.
Speaking is one access route: drawing, pointing and objects can communicate the
same reason. Do not assess accent, English fluency or handwriting as arithmetic.

M01–M16 are proposed book-navigation slots, not new curriculum IDs. Only M01
has this new complete pilot; existing first-unit lessons support M01/M02.
All other module rows are authoring specifications, not completed lessons or tests.
Outcome numbers below abbreviate the existing O-MATH-001-NNN definitions.

The [course prerequisite table](README.md#proposed-unit-progression) governs
sequencing. A book module may need several lessons, review or split sittings.
Sixteen modules do not specify sixteen weeks or equal teaching time.
The astronomy modules need actual observation opportunities and reviewed safety/
access arrangements before use; no sky records or cycles are fabricated here.

## Review cadence and assessment

Propose cumulative review slots after M04, M08, M12 and M16. This placement is our
design choice. Each combines a short independent application, a model/explanation,
a teacher's outcome-level judgment and a learner reflection. Actual tasks and
keys for those four cumulative checks remain to author before administration.

- After M04: taught portions of 001–006 plus parity introduction; equal
  grouping/sharing, geometry and astronomy remain unassessed.
- After M08: add the taught components of 007–009, 012 and 013; record any
  larger-calculation components still pending.
- After M12: include taught 010–011 and the larger-calculation work actually taught
  in M11; do not infer a full astronomy outcome from a supplied table alone.
- After M16: report evidence separately for all thirteen outcomes and scope;
  the selected project cannot automatically cover everything.

Keep the existing unit assessment between M01/M02 and later studies. A pilot
cycle check is formative and never replaces that assessment. Distinguish
independent, supported, needs review and not assessed. Never combine them into
a total-score claim of understanding. Timed drills remain optional fluency practice.

## Route and time discipline

PV2 stays within 0–99; PV3 within 0–999 after the hundreds prerequisites.
PVX remains a separately taught optional thousand supplement. Do not put a
hundreds task into a PV2 page to fit the book layout.
Number names and arithmetic vocabulary support meaning, with read-aloud and
spoken/drawn responses available.

The M01 pilot is a two-session replacement for the 25-minute L-MATH-001-01-01
implementation: 2 × 25 = 50 planned minutes, an additional 25 minutes overall.
It does not add two sessions on top of the original lesson. The optional expanded
PV2 route therefore plans 150 rather than 125 core minutes; expanded PV3 plans
175 rather than 150, with lesson 2's hundreds work still required. Workbook and
formative review time are separately stated in the pilot. These are adjustable
authoring estimates, not an agreed schedule or measured delivery times.
