## Purpose and starting point

Develop understanding of number and its relationships, then investigate form,
measure, and sky cycles. Practice accurate language, reasons, and clear
explanations throughout. Actual starting skills are unknown; use the
[entry check](../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/entry-check.md) to choose review and pace.

## Scope and progression

Arithmetic begins with unit, whole, amount, order, composition, difference, and
numerical relationships. Geometry develops magnitude, form, and measurement.
Astronomy connects
number and measurement to observed sky cycles. Modern everyday problems and
representations support these studies.

The course outline is not a claim that all units are ready. Only the first study
has draft lessons and tasks. Refine ranges, examples, and duration after entry work.
