# Composition, difference, and parity

- ID: U-MATH-001-02
- Course: [C-MATH-001](../../README.md)
- Status: draft
- Art: arithmetic
- Prerequisites: counting named one-units and taught components of O-MATH-001-001 and O-MATH-001-004
- Scope: whole numbers 0–20, no negative results or larger place-value procedures
- Proposed time: four 25-minute sessions, plus flexible entry and unit check; no calendar set

Use identical counters, blank paper and pencil. Name units and symbols (grammar), explain how parts and totals relate (logic), and communicate a reason to someone else (rhetoric). Use worked-example fading from model to guided completion to fresh independent work.

## Entry and route choice

Run the [session generator](learner-practice.md) with `--activity entry --limit 10`.
Observe one-to-one counting, comparison with a reason, and composing a ten from
ten ones. Use the generated key. Supply counters and retain read-aloud, oral,
drawn, pointed or scribed responses without answer cues. If counting or comparison
is insecure, model and practice smaller rows before retrying with a fresh entry
session. Use the lesson activities with `--limit 10` as the smaller-range review
route; keep that evidence separate. If composing a ten is insecure, revisit
Unit 1 before extending past 10.
