# Choose a calculation for a story

- ID: L-MATH-001-02-03
- Status: draft
- Art: arithmetic
- Outcome: O-MATH-001-006
- Prerequisite: evidence from lesson 02
- Materials: identical counters, paper and pencil
- Proposed time: 25 minutes, adjustable

## Grammar, logic and rhetoric

Name the counted unit and the unknown. Distinguish a change in one collection from comparing two collections. Explain why the model answers the question.

## Teaching sequence and key

1. Model (6 min): A box holds 8 pencils; add 5. Join two groups: 13 pencils. Contrast with two rows of 8 and 5 pencils: the unmatched 3 show how many more, with neither row changed.
2. Guided completion (7 min): There are 12 beads; 4 are removed. Model what changes and name the unit. **Key:** 8 beads remain; cross out 4 of 12.
3. Independent and exit (12 min, flexible): use a new [generated learner session](../learner-practice.md) with activity `stories` and the taught limit. Use its matching teacher key for feedback and select a fresh session for reassessment.

## Evidence and response

Look for the keyed result, a matching model and the stated reason. If the result is right but the reason or model is missing, revisit the concrete example and use a fresh task. Fade solution cues only after a correct fresh response and reason; retain all access supports listed in the [unit](../README.md). Do not record a prompted solution as independent.
