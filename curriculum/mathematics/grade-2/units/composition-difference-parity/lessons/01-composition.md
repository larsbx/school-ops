# Parts and wholes

- ID: L-MATH-001-02-01
- Status: draft
- Art: arithmetic
- Outcome: O-MATH-001-005
- Prerequisite: unit entry check
- Materials: identical counters, paper and pencil
- Proposed time: 25 minutes, adjustable

## Grammar, logic and rhetoric

Name part, whole and equal. Explain that moving objects between parts keeps the whole fixed. Tell a partner how both arrangements count the same collection.

## Teaching sequence and key

1. Model (6 min): Join 6 and 3 counters: 6 + 3 = 9. Move one across the divider: 5 + 4 = 9. Count each part and the unchanged whole.
2. Guided completion (7 min): Build 7 + 4, then move one counter across. Name both parts and whole. **Key:** 7 + 4 = 6 + 5 = 11; no counters entered or left.
3. Independent and exit (12 min, flexible): use a new [generated learner session](../learner-practice.md) with activity `composition` and the taught limit. Use its matching teacher key for feedback and select a fresh session for reassessment.
