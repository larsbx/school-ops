# Difference and inverse checks

- ID: L-MATH-001-02-02
- Status: draft
- Art: arithmetic
- Outcome: O-MATH-001-005
- Prerequisite: evidence from lesson 01
- Materials: identical counters, paper and pencil
- Proposed time: 25 minutes, adjustable

## Grammar, logic and rhetoric

Name difference and missing part. Test subtraction by reconstructing the whole. Explain the check without relying on the symbol alone.

## Teaching sequence and key

1. Model (6 min): Build 14 counters, mark 9 and separate them. Count 5 left. Check 9 + 5 = 14.
2. Guided completion (7 min): Separate 6 from 12. Complete the reconstruction equation. **Key:** 6 remain; 6 + 6 = 12.
3. Independent and exit (12 min, flexible): use a new [generated learner session](../learner-practice.md) with activity `difference` and the taught limit. Use its matching teacher key for feedback and select a fresh session for reassessment.

## Evidence and response

Look for the keyed result, a matching model and the stated reason. If the result is right but the reason or model is missing, revisit the concrete example and use a fresh task. Fade solution cues only after a correct fresh response and reason; retain all access supports listed in the [unit](../README.md). Do not record a prompted solution as independent.
