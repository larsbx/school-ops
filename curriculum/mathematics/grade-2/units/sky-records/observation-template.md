# Safe, accessible sky record — blank learner form (DRAFT)

This **blank** form is for **actual, voluntary, supervised/approved** observations of a safe sky feature; it contains no fabricated student records. The record remains **private to the class/learner** and must not be committed to this shared repository. A school-verified source packet with its source, timestamp, type and license can be an alternate route when direct safe observation is inaccessible; **no packet or source approval is supplied here**.

**Before use:** an adult must select a safe, ordinary observation situation and demonstrate its accessibility. **Never look directly at the Sun, even through clouds or through binoculars, telescopes, cameras or other optics.** Do not require evening/night outings, travel, exact location disclosure, personal photos, outdoor access during unsafe conditions or observation when nothing is visible. Classroom observation from a window or approved historical records can be used when appropriate. Do not infer a learner's identity, family practices or location.
