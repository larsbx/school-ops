# Episode S5-L4 — Tables, diagrams and the limits of prediction (DRAFT)

- **Unit/outcome:** [U-MATH-001-05](../README.md), O-MATH-001-011; revisit O010 honest no-view and intervals.
- **Evidence before starting:** E3 reading row/column labels; relevant E1 arithmetic and E2 distinctions, checked rather than assumed.
- **Art/Trivium:** arithmetic counts and astronomy-source caution; **grammar** rows, columns, categories, count, scale; **logic** rejects “blocked = absent”; **rhetoric** presents a diagram and its limit.
- **Provisional duration/materials:** 25 minutes; equal-sized counters or large-print/tactile bar chart, labels “clear,” “blocked,” “not attempted,” original fictional sheet and independent exit.
- **Independent exit:** X4 with [teacher-only rubric](../teacher-guide.md#practice-and-lesson-exit-keys).

## Teaching episode

| Phase | Minutes | Actual task / teacher move |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Retrieve | 4 | Read one March record; point to which cell shows the *view condition* rather than Moon evidence. |
| Model | 6 | Use a **separate invented** two-day log: “view clear” twice and “view blocked” once. Make equal-scale bars 2 and 1, label the bars and explain that neither length measures Moon phase. |
| Guided/fade | 7 | Learner tackles **P4**: count clear (3) and blocked (1) from March 2/4/7/11. Teacher initially marks first tally, then removes marking for the remaining records and learner constructs equal-unit bars. |
| Logic/rhetoric | 4 | Challenge **P5** (“not checked” means “Moon absent”). Require revision supported by a cell. Learner explains their chart to a partner using the category units. |
| Independent | 4 | Independently solve **X4**, a distinct 3/2/1 category scenario, and say one thing the counts **cannot** determine. |

## Model, feedback and progression

**Misconception:** a longer bar for “clear views” means the Moon was present more days. The table measures view opportunities, not lunar visibility; correct the measured variable. **Second misconception:** multiple equal-size bars can use varying scale units. Rebuild with a repeated consistent counter unit; don't change chart scale mid-task.

**Candidate exit criterion:** correct categories **3 clear + 2 blocked + 1 not attempted = 6** and explicit uncertainty for blocked dates. A bar or tactile array with equal-size units, oral explanation or scribed response is acceptable. Continue to [draft transfer check](../../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/sky-records-learner.md) **only after** entry and prior exits have been independently demonstrated; real-observation strand remains separately gated.
