# Episode S5-L1 — What does a sky record actually say? (DRAFT)

- **Unit/outcomes:** [U-MATH-001-05](../README.md), O-MATH-001-010 (what is and is not observed), O-MATH-001-011 (record categories).
- **Evidence before starting:** Unit E2 and E3 on [learner sheet](../practice-learner.md#entry-e1e4) demonstrated or remediated. No actual sky observation is presumed.
- **Art/Trivium:** astronomy; **grammar** distinguishes “seen,” “not seen in this view,” “not checked”; **logic** tests whether cloud-blocked means absent; **rhetoric** gives a checkable correction to a peer.
- **Provisional duration/materials:** 25 minutes, original fictional table, paper/large-print or tactile cells, voice/pointing/AAC options. No binoculars, night outing, physical observation or web imagery required for this rehearsal.
- **Independent exit:** X1 on [learner sheet](../practice-learner.md#lesson-exits-x1x4); model answer **teacher-only** in [teacher guide](../teacher-guide.md#practice-and-lesson-exit-keys).

## Teaching episode

| Phase | Minutes | Actual task / teacher move |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Retrieve | 3 | Say or show the difference between “a pupil saw a marker” and “a curtain prevented checking the marker.” Ask what the second sentence does **not** assert. |
| Model | 6 | Introduce a **fictional** observation notebook with three columns: date, view condition, what was recorded. Teacher uses a *different* toy example: “Window covered, did not attempt to observe.” Put `not checked`, not `absent`, in the outcome cell. |
| Guided and faded | 7 | Use March 2/4/7 rows from the [original learner table](../practice-learner.md#entry-e1e4); read a row, locate date and category, then hide teacher's highlighting and let the pupil locate the next row. Name exactly which column supports each answer. |
| Logic dialogue | 5 | A friend claims “no observation of the Moon means the Moon is gone.” Ask for a counterexample involving clouds/roof and distinguish “not seen” from “not checked.” Require evidence, not a personal opinion about the Moon. |
| Independent | 4 | Answer **X1** independently by words, objects, pointing or drawing. Privately note whether the answer distinguishes no-view from negative sighting **and gives the obstruction as the reason**. |

## Model, feedback and progression

The teacher models the neutral **curtain** counterexample without revealing X1. For a confused learner, provide two separate boxes, “view attempted” and “what seen,” and retry with a new covered-window scenario; do not merely circle the right answer. A correct statement is limited by viewpoint, time and recorded conditions. A log does not certify a student's memory or an external source.

**Candidate exit criterion:** classify X1 as **no-view** and explain *why* it cannot be called a sighting or negative sighting. If unsupported, revisit E2/E3 before Episode 2. This task is fictional and **does not** count as a real astronomical observation.
