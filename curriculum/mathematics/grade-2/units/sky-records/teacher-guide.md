## Entry keys

- **E1:** March 3 → 4 (1), 4 → 5 (2), 5 → 6 (3): **3 intervals**, although four date labels appear. Miscount → physically walk gaps between dated boxes.
- **E2:** (a) **reported observation**, (b) **no-view/missing evidence**, (c) **prediction**; (a) does not externally authenticate the person's claim.
- **E3:** March 4 **view blocked / not checked**; March 7 **Moon seen/recorded**. March 2 is **not seen from that clear view**, not proof of Moon absence.
- **E4:** **4 rods** of length 2 cover length 8: `2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8`; number of rods differs from count of short units. Verify gap-free measurement, not bare answer.

**Entry policy:** sample each gate independently; if E4 fails, do not treat Unit 4 PR #40 as mastery. Use tactile boxes, enlarged tables, AAC and scripted spoken dates where needed. Retest with a parallel case not the same memorized response.

## Fictional source cards and reading script

The [P-table](practice-learner.md#practice-p1p6) explicitly invents a group of records all at **16:00** on March 2, 4, 7 and 11, 2026. These are **not actual astronomy source data**. The days are chosen solely to rehearse unit counting, status categories and reasoning. Use the words:
> A **date** is a calendar label. A **day interval** is one step between successive dates. “Not seen” reports only the limited field of view, whereas “not checked” supplies no Moon observation. Neither proves the Moon is absent.

Model a *different* arithmetic example: March 1 → 3 is 2 days. Ask the learner to explain the **unit** and count the spaces between labels, not both endpoint labels.

## Source, access and acceptance gates

1. **Real data provenance (NOT MET):** no historical sky facts or images are supplied or verified by this draft. Before real-observation evidence, choose a safely observable feature; if inaccessible, choose an independently reviewed, **dated** astronomical record/images with traceable organization, timestamp, content, rights and conditions. A search-result snippet is not reviewed primary data. Verify the selected source and any redistribution/license terms.
2. **Safety/access (NOT MET):** teacher/owner must confirm school setting, supervision, weather alternative, permitted observation time, protective no-direct-Sun practice and AAC/tactile/high-contrast/translation needs. No learner must travel at night or share an address.
3. **Observational evidence (NOT MET):** two genuinely dated, source-checked records on distinct dates, with conditions, a measured interval, careful limits and a justified comparison are **proposed** for O010. No observation is invented; a no-view entry is an honest datum but not physical-cycle proof. A verified historical packet may satisfy access-equivalent evidence if properly reviewed.
4. **Assessment security (NOT MET):** reference prompts and teacher answers are public; author genuinely independent parallel assessment forms before grading. Use [teacher rubric](../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/sky-records-teacher.md) and do not infer mastery from a numeric total or optional timed drill.
5. **Curriculum authority (NOT MET):** Unit 4/008 prerequisite, scope, real time budget, textbook/source selection, support accommodations, criterion thresholds, pedagogical and scientific suitability need owner review. Neither GitHub CI nor a merged PR grants `ready`.

**Privacy:** Only original reusable *blank* materials reside in Git. Store student records, dates of observation tied to a learner, individual responses, grades, attendance and class links in the private school system. Do not commit record packets containing learner identifiers.

## Teacher decision / progress labels

- **Rehearsed:** student can reason with fictional source-marked examples; does not establish real observation.
- **Source-checked observed:** independent dated records, correct comparisons and appropriately limited cycle claim, after source/safety review.
- **Needs prerequisite review:** one or more E gates absent or unsupported; teach again and recheck with fresh variant.
- **Unassessed:** resource inaccessible or no suitable real data/observation opportunity; never replace with an invented success.

Keep these per-outcome and per-subskill, not a single total score.
