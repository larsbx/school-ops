# U-MATH-001-05 — Sky records, intervals and cautious cycles (DRAFT)

- **Existing reserved unit:** U-MATH-001-05 of [C-MATH-001](../../README.md), not a new astronomy course or a new stable identifier.
- **Status:** draft, not ready or classroom-approved; lessons, reference assessment and teacher answers are **authored**, while real observation sources, teaching arrangements and independent variants are **not reviewed**.
- **Intended audience:** Grade 2 after demonstrated entry skills, not assumed from the grade label.
- **Primary mathematical art:** astronomy through dated observations, interval arithmetic and tabulation; the Trivium is substantive in each lesson. Music is excluded.
- **Existing outcomes:** [O-MATH-001-010 and O-MATH-001-011](../../README.md#course-outcomes); prerequisite checks revisit 002 and 008, with date-step counting and consistent units. This unit does **not** certify 002 or 008.
- **Dependency caution:** measurement evidence (008) is being authored in [Unit 4 PR #40](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/pull/40); a PR, even if merged, does not prove that any learner has been taught or demonstrated it.
- **Draft allocation:** 4 × 25-minute lessons, a separate untimed entry check and an untimed transfer check; actual class dates, resources, calendar and teaching-time budget are **unassigned**.
- **Limits:** no unverified claim about lunar phase periods, sunrise/sunset, time zones, historical weather, or a supplied classroom observation. The public tables in this package are **invented exercise data**, emphatically not records of the sky on the printed dates.

## Entry evidence and remedial routes

Give [E1–E4 learner prompts](practice-learner.md#entry-e1e4) and consult the [separate key](teacher-guide.md#entry-keys). Record the evidence privately by condition, never a single average.

| Gate | Candidate evidence (requires owner calibration) | If unmet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 date counting (002) | Correctly count the number of **day intervals** from March 3 to March 6, not inclusive endpoint labels | Walk across drawn labeled day boxes and retry with another pair |
| E2 records (011 preparation) | Distinguish an observation, a no-view record and a future guess in three brief statements | Model “I saw” vs “I could not see” vs “I think” on neutral classroom examples |
| E3 table reading (011 preparation) | Locate the one “view blocked” row and one “Moon recorded” row in an original practice table | Trace row/column intersection with tactile or large-print grid |
| E4 equal measurement units (008) | Show that an 8-short-unit strip takes four 2-short-unit rods, with no gaps/overlap | Revisit Unit 4's checked measurement work; do not pretend draft authoring establishes it |

A learner may work on neutral records while the physical measurement prerequisite is remediated, but **full unit evidence cannot be claimed** without the relevant prerequisites. Reading dates by an adult, pointing, AAC, braille/large-print/tactile charts, dictation and translated classroom directions are access supports; do not supply the inference, count or counterexample as a cue.

## Proposed decision (not mastery)

For **O010**, require independent accurate interval/count reasoning (reference Tasks S5-02, S5-03, S5-05), clear rejection of unsupported periodicity (S5-07), **and** a genuinely dated, source-checked observation comparison (S5-09, externally gated). For **O011**, require a correctly labeled representation (S5-04 and S5-06), and differentiation of recorded data from a prediction (S5-01 and S5-07). Explain errors before rechecking with unseen equivalent tasks. The thresholds require owner and teacher review; no summed score can mask a missing component.
