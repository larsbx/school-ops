# Lesson 1 — Regroup a larger collection (teacher guide)

**Unit:** [U-MATH-001-06](../README.md) · **Status:** draft, not teaching-ready · **Outcomes:** O-MATH-001-006 and bounded 007 · **Art:** arithmetic · **Pattern:** worked-example fading with inverse checking · **Proposed duration:** 25 minutes, flexible.

**Prerequisite:** independent E1/E2 from [entry diagnostic](../practice-learner.md); otherwise use smaller quantities and do not administer the 0–199 reference check. **Materials/access:** base-ten bundles, counter drawing, paper, verbal/AAC/pointing/scribed responses; read problems aloud without supplying the operation.

## Trivium and activity

**Grammar (4 min).** A *one* counts one object, a *ten* groups ten ones, a *hundred* ten tens. Distinguish **pencils** (counted unit) from **tens** (counting unit). Learner identifies the two known collections and the unknown combined total.

**Logic/model (8 min).** Teacher says: “A cupboard contains **68 pencils** and a new box adds **47 pencils**. How many pencils are there altogether?” Show 6 tens 8 ones + 4 tens 7 ones. The 15 ones become 1 ten and 5 ones; then 11 tens become 1 hundred and 1 ten. **Answer 115 pencils**. The bundles change representation, not the physical count. Check 115 − 47 = 68. Avoid claiming a written algorithm is necessary.

**Faded guided work (6 min).** “There are **56** wooden tiles and **37** more. How many altogether?” Give empty tens/ones chart, not filled digits. **Key: 93 tiles**; 6 + 7 = 13 ones, so 1 extra ten and 3 ones; 5 + 3 + 1 = 9 tens. Check 93 − 37 = 56.

**Independent exit (7 min).** Present [P1](../practice-learner.md#p1--independent-regrouping) without the guide. **Key: 75 + 28 = 103 paper cards**, with 13 ones exchanged for 1 ten and 3 ones, and ten tens exchanged for a hundred. Require a model/description, final amount/unit and an inverse or recomposition reason. Allow counter/tactile model and normal access supports.

## Feedback/recheck

If the learner gives 93 or 103 without explaining the exchange, ask for a representation on a guided *different* quantity, then independently recheck a new equivalent task. If 0–199 is not secure, review 0–99 sums with tens/ones; **do not record independent evidence of the hundreds route**. Record no learner responses in Git. Grammar (unit labels), logic (unchanged count) and rhetoric (explanation another learner reconstructs) must each be visible.
