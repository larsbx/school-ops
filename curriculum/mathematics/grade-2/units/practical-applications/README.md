# Applications: calculations, tables and defensible choices

- **Unit:** `U-MATH-001-06` — an **already reserved** unit of [C-MATH-001](../../README.md).
- **Record/status:** drafted unit with actual tasks, keys and checks; **draft, not teaching-ready**.
- **Audience:** intended Grade 2, after independent prerequisite evidence; no course-wide or learner readiness claim.
- **Quadrivium:** arithmetic is primary; number-line geometry and representing measured magnitudes are supporting applications. Tables of supplied counts extend only the **count/table component** of astronomy-linked O-MATH-001-011; they do **not** assess sky observation or recurrence. **Music excluded.**
- **Existing course outcomes:** `O-MATH-001-006` (models/units of practical stories), `O-MATH-001-007` (bounded place-value calculations), `O-MATH-001-009` (equal-scale number-line steps), `O-MATH-001-011` (tables/limits of inference, **counts only**). Incidental 008 and 012 examples are **not** independent evidence of measuring/form.
- **Proposed route:** 0–199, only after demonstrating hundreds/place-value work and prerequisite number-line/table interpretation. Alternative 0–99 *instructional review* route is available, but the full reference assessment here is for 0–199. No decimals, fractions, negative numbers, percentages, time-speed formulae or formal algorithms required.
- **Estimated instructional time:** four flexible 25-minute lessons; entry diagnostic and independent check separate. Not an agreed schedule or timed fluency criterion.
- **Materials/access:** reusable 10-rod/1-counter or drawn place-value chart, blank equal-spaced number line, grid/paper, and optional tactile/large-print counters. Read-aloud, pointing, speech, drawing, signing, AAC and adult transcription allowed **without numeric or reasoning cues**.

The existing [course progression](../../README.md#proposed-unit-progression) and [course evidence plan](../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/course-evidence-plan.md) govern scope. This unit authors the existing M11–M13 application slots from [the optional book map](../../course-design.md#sixteen-module-book-plan-within-seven-units), **not** three new course units. It does not overwrite or claim completed teaching of Units 4–5: their geometry and astronomy material is under separate PR review as of 2026-11-06.
