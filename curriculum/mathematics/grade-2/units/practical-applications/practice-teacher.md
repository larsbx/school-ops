# Unit 6 — teacher key, gates and remediation

**Draft teacher-only companion** to [learner entry and practice](practice-learner.md) in [U-MATH-001-06](README.md). Do not distribute this file or any completed learner records in learner handouts. All examples are original, fixed and already exposed; each new *independent* administration requires fresh, checked numbers and a matching key. Evidence and accommodations for individual learners belong in private systems.

## Prerequisite entry key and gates

| Check | Complete key/model | Gate and remediation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E1** | 48 = **4 tens and 8 ones**. One more ten produces **58 = 5 tens 8 ones**; 8 single ones are unchanged. | Must correctly name tens and ones and explain adding ten changes only the ten count. If not, re-model tens/ones from [Unit 1](../place-value/README.md); recheck with 37 + 10 = 47, not the exposed 48. |
| **E2** | 6 + 7 = **13 counters**; two disjoint groups or a counting-on model, with named units. 13 − 7 = 6 verifies. | Correct result, distinct groups and a reason. If not, review [Unit 2](../composition-difference-parity/README.md); recheck 5 + 8 = 13. |
| **E3** | 24 + 7 = **31**, with **seven equal one-unit steps** or an equivalent uniform tactile line; endpoints 24 and 31. | Scale and distance must both be defensible. If not, practice 20→26 (+6) and recheck independently before the number-line lesson. |
| **E4** | Day B is greatest at **7 cards**. No Day D observation is supplied; its count is **unknown** from these rows. | Correct comparison plus express uncertainty. If not, point to exactly which rows are supplied, then recheck new rows 6, 8, 6 and ask about Day D. |

**Routes:** E1/E2 precede lesson 1; E1–E3 precede lesson 2; E4 precedes lesson 3; all four independently checked precede integrated lesson 4. Teacher support during a task is recorded as **supported**, not independent. Keep read-aloud, tactile, AAC and scribing supports distinct from answer cues. Use the 0–99 review route if hundreds work is not established, but do **not** report independent 0–199 O-MATH-001-007 evidence.

## Guided/independent practice keys

| Task | Calculation/model | Complete response and reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1** | 75 + 28 = **103** | 5 + 8 = 13 ones = 1 ten + 3 ones; 7 + 2 + 1 = 10 tens = 1 hundred; **103 paper cards**. Check 103 − 28 = 75. |
| **P2** | 70 + 25 = 95; 95 − 13 = **82** | Uniform scale shows **70 → 95 → 82**. Final 82 is above 70 by 12, below 95 by 13. |
| **P3** | 7 + 11 + 9 = **27**; 11 − 7 = **4** | **27 recycled cards**; middle exceeds first by **4 cards**. No count for “next day” exists: **13 is unsupported**, not necessarily false. |
| **P4** | A: 4×7 = 28; 28 − 25 = 3. B: 3×9 = 27; 27 − 25 = 2. | **Both sufficient**; **B leaves 2 extras instead of 3**, hence fewer extras under stated assumptions. Cannot compare cost without price. |
| **P5** | 66 + 27 = **93** | 6 + 7 = 13 ones, exchange 10 ones for 1 ten; 6 + 2 + 1 = 9 tens; **93 tiles**. 93 − 27 = 66. |
| **P6** | 118 − 49 = **69** | 49 + 69 = 118; **69 books remain**. A counted-on inverse: 49→50 (+1), 50→100 (+50), 100→118 (+18); total 69. |
| **P7** | 10 + 8 + 15 = **33**; 15 − 8 = **7** | **33 objects** across A–C; C exceeds B by **7**. D was not reported. |
| **P8** | A: 2×13 = 26, **2 extras**; B: 3×9 = 27, **3 extras** | Both meet 24; **A** leaves fewer extras even though it has fewer packs. Compare actual capacity, not pack count. |

## Feedback, evidence and next steps

Check separately: (i) quantity/units; (ii) representation, including **equal unit spacing** for a line; (iii) justification by regrouping, inverse, supplied record or explicit assumption; (iv) communication reconstructible by a peer. For tables, do not accept predictions as observed facts. An accurate answer with substantive assistance is recorded **supported**, never independent. A failure of E3 or E4 must not be hidden by a high arithmetic score. Use the [separate learner reference assessment](../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/applications-learner.md) only after teacher review; [its rubric/key](../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/applications-teacher.md) requires each assessed component. All proficiency decisions, instructional pacing and accessibility suitability remain human judgments; **no status promotion**.
