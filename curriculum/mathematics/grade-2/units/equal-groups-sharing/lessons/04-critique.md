# Episode 4 — Critique, transfer and evidence

- Lesson ID: **L-MATH-001-03-04**; **status: draft**; arithmetic / O-MATH-001-013 (integrated grouping/sharing/doubling/halving).
- Prerequisite: Episode 1–3 targeted evidence or remediation, not merely attendance.
- Materials: counters, blank boxes/grids, learner-only independent form; proposed 25 minutes (flexible), separate untimed check.

## Trivium actions

**Grammar:** read *group count*, *objects per group*, *total*, *double*, *half*, *leftover* with the correct units.

**Logic:** look for a missing counter or an unequal group when testing an asserted product or quotient. Rotating a full rectangular array preserves its count but swaps descriptions of rows and columns.

**Rhetoric:** give a precise claim, one model, one reason and a check or counterexample another learner can perform.

## Teaching and independently checkable work

1. **Review (5 min):** Show 4 rows of 3 counters. Rotate to 3 rows of 4. **Key:** 12 objects either way; 4 × 3 = 3 × 4 = 12 because the same 12 counters were rearranged, not added.
2. **Find the flaw (6 min):** A fictional speaker says 4 boxes containing 5, 5, 5 and **4** stones show “4 groups of 5 = 20.” **Key:** only 19 stones are shown; the groups are not equal; one stone is missing. The *claim about groups as drawn* is false even though 4 × 5 = 20 is true.
3. **Independent explanation rehearsal (6 min):** Ask for two ways to use 18 counters: “3 equal groups” and “groups of 3.” **Key:** 3 groups of 6 versus 6 groups of 3; the number 6 answers different questions. Accept equivalent verbal, tactile, diagrammatic or AAC explanations.
4. **Unit check (separate untimed sitting):** Provide only [learner form](../../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/equal-groups-sharing-learner.md); use the [private teacher rubric](../../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/equal-groups-sharing-teacher.md) afterward. These are six fixed *draft* prompts; after exposure do not reuse as an unseen summative form. A total score cannot replace component judgments.

On incomplete component evidence, reteach the smallest missing relationship and use a new checked example. Keep individual evidence and any accommodations in private storage.
