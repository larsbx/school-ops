# U-MATH-001-04 — Form, magnitude and measurement (draft)

- **Existing reserved unit:** U-MATH-001-04 in [C-MATH-001](../../README.md); **status: draft, not teaching-ready**.
- **Audience:** learners for whom the entry evidence is demonstrated; “Grade 2” is not evidence of mastery.
- **Primary art:** geometry, using arithmetic for counting units; grammar, logic and rhetoric occur in every lesson. Music is excluded.
- **Outcome targets:** [O-MATH-001-008, 009, 012](../../README.md#course-outcomes). This unit reviews 004, 005, and the *equal-group component* of 013; it does **not** certify O013 or whole-course mastery.
- **Dependencies:** existing Unit 1 comparison and Unit 2 composition/difference; equal-square grouping from Unit 3 must be checked **when its draft is reviewed and taught**. [Unit 3 PR #39](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/pull/39) is not presumed merged, approved or completed.
- **Provisional time:** four teaching episodes of roughly 25 minutes each, plus flexible entry and unit evidence; actual calendar, session length and materials require owner approval.
- **Number and measure boundary:** whole counts 0–20, equal-size square tiles, 1-unit and 2-unit length rods, straight marked lines 0–12. Do not infer multiplication algorithms, area formulae, fractional notation, real-world unit conversions or an astronomical investigation.

This is **Unit 4 within the existing mathematics course**, not a separate Grade 2 geometry course. The [Grade 2 geometry planning scaffold](../../../../scaffolds/grade-2/geometry.md) remains a proposal for *separate* future scope and cannot supersede C-MATH-001. In the optional [book map](../../course-design.md), this unit develops M07 (form and equal parts) and M08 (measure and locate).

## Entry, accessibility and prerequisite route

Administer [E1–E4 learner prompts](practice-learner.md#entry-e1e4) with the separately stored [key and feedback](practice-teacher.md#entry-key-e1e4). Keep performance for each condition separately; no averaged score substitutes for missing components.

| Condition | Observable gate (candidate, unvalidated) | If not demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 — quantity and comparison (004, 005 preparation) | Count 8 counters, compare 8 with 6 and explain with matched pairs | Revisit Unit 1 counting/comparison and Unit 2 small compositions; retest with fresh objects |
| E2 — equal-square grouping (013 prerequisite only) | Build two distinct arrangements of 6 equal tiles, preserving count | Model one-to-one counting and Unit 3 equal grouping if available; do not assume #39 is accepted |
| E3 — ordering/spacing | Identify 2 and 5 on evenly spaced ticks 0–6; show three *intervals* from 2 to 5 | Trace each step physically; build equal gaps with paper strips and retry |
| E4 — selecting a length unit | Cover a 6-short-unit strip using six identical 1-unit rods, starting at one end without gaps/overlap | Directly model one rod per span; avoid counting marks as spans |

All four must be independently demonstrated for the full draft route. Otherwise use supported practice at the demonstrated scale and defer conclusions. For visual/motor access, offer large-print/tactile grids, raised counters, pre-drawn uniformly spaced lines, pointer/AAC responses, oral/scribed explanations and nonvisual counting of tile slots. These supports **do not disclose solutions, change unit sizes mid-measurement or remove the reasoning criterion**. Record support and entry observations in a private student system only.
