# G4-L2 — Measure with named, repeated units

- **Unit:** [U-MATH-001-04](../README.md) · **status: draft** · **provisional 25 minutes**.
- **Art and outcome:** geometry; O-MATH-001-008, introductory comparison of magnitudes.
- **Entry:** E1, E4; O005 counting/composition; E2 reviewed as needed.
- **Materials:** paper strip exactly 12 short-unit lengths, identical 1-unit rods and 2-unit rods (each long rod equals two short), tactile endpoints if appropriate.
- **Observable goal:** cover one strip without gaps or overlap by two coherent unit choices, give both counts with their unit names, explain the inverse relation between chosen unit length and count.
- **Grammar:** length, endpoint, unit, 1-short-unit rod, 2-short-unit rod, repeated placement, gap, overlap.
- **Logic:** refute the claim “6 rods always means the strip is shorter than 12 rods” when rods have different lengths.
- **Rhetoric:** tell another learner how to start at the endpoint, place and count repeats, and why the resulting numbers differ.

## Instruction and evidence

1. **Retrieve (3 min):** E4 cover 6 short units; do not start with numerical conversion rules.
2. **Demonstrate (7 min):** align 12 single rods from the left endpoint; record **12 short units**. Replace with six double-length rods laid end-to-end; record **6 double units**. The *same* strip remains unchanged.
3. **Check a false claim (5 min):** teacher places 5 double rods with a 2-short-unit gap. Ask if saying “5 double units long” is valid. Learner identifies gap and restores the sixth rod.
4. **Fade (5 min):** cover an 8-short-unit strip in fours of the double unit; learner describes “8 short units = 4 double units” with no algorithmic formula required.
5. **Independent exit (5 min):** [L2-X1](../practice-learner.md#lesson-exits); check named unit, no gaps and reason using [teacher key](../practice-teacher.md#lesson-exit-keys).

## Feedback

A numeral without its unit is incomplete. If the learner compares 12 and 6 as *unlike lengths* without naming unit size, put the same strip under both runs and elicit the explanation. If motor placement is not accessible, use tactile slots and a pointing/voice selection route, retaining gap/overlap reasoning. No external ruler conversion, fractions or perimeter calculation is asserted.
