# Grade 2 place-value teaching pack

C-MATH-001 / U-MATH-001-01 • draft • authoring review 2026-10-27

This pack assembles the existing six lesson records into two separately taught
routes. It reuses the merged M01 pilot without changing its content or identifiers.
It is an authored teaching pack, not a claim of learner starting skills or readiness.
The primary Quadrivium art is arithmetic: decimal units, conserved quantities,
repeated addition and numerical order. Grammar, logic and rhetoric are substantive
parts of every session. Geometry and astronomy retain their later course work;
no music or standards alignment is added.

## Choose and prepare

Begin with the [entry teacher record](../../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/entry-check.md)
and its [separate learner sheet](../../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/entry-tasks.md).
Use its per-skill evidence and M01's twelve-counter check. A difficulty counting
through 100 in entry item 2 does not block the PV2 route; it is diagnostic only.
If one-to-one counting is insecure, teach smaller collections before this fixed
pilot. Do not silently change task ranges and call the altered form reviewed.

Entry adds 10–15 minutes. M01 workbook adds an optional 10–16; its formative
review adds an optional 5–10. These are separate from the core budgets. Optional
fluency adds locally chosen time. The 25-minute lesson-6 estimate includes up to
20 minutes for the core check; allow extra time or split sittings for models and
explanations. No completion deadline applies. Weekly allocation, actual duration
and dates remain undecided. Do not teach both the ordinary lesson 1 and M01 as
unbudgeted duplicate instruction.

For PVX only after PV3, retain the [thousand grouping activity](../lessons/03-number-forms.md#optional-thousand-extension)
(25 proposed minutes), [boundary activity](../lessons/04-counting.md#optional-count-through-1000)
(10–15), and separate PVX check (5–10). Total additional estimate: 40–50 minutes.
PVX does not become a core requirement.

## Materials and access checklist

- Both routes: paper, pencil, blank Tens/Ones chart, individual counters and
  inspectable ten-groups; enough base-ten equivalents for 99; two separate partner cards.
- PV3: blank Hundreds/Tens/Ones chart and inspectable hundred-groups, enough
  base-ten equivalents for 999. Establish one hundred as ten tens, each ten as ten ones.
- PVX only: ten established hundred-groups, enclosing thousand-group or established
  thousand block, and blank four-column chart. A symbol without an established value
  cannot demonstrate a concrete exchange.
- Each learner: learner sheets and working paper; partner cards distributed one at
  a time. A teacher can be the partner. No device or online service is required.
- Directions can be read aloud; accept spoken, drawn, pointed or scribed responses.
  Keep units and reasoning criteria intact. Spelling and reading speed are not criteria.
- Confirm print size, legibility, manipulatives, access supports and actual availability
  locally before teaching. Required assessment designs remain draft.

## Bank integration and decisions

Use the corrected [bank version 1.0.1](../../../../../../bank/place-value/README.md)
with the [route administration workflow](bank-workflow.md). It generates independent
PV2/PV3 scopes, no PVX. Timed fluency is optional practice; its speed or completion
never replaces mathematical understanding. Conceptual forms use the same
[A-MATH-001-02 criteria](../../../../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/place-value-check.md#outcome-criteria).
Inspect every generated learner/key pair; seed changes do not guarantee fresh prompts.

For each outcome, record independent / supported / needs review / not assessed
privately, with route, task/form ID and support. Use the guide's follow-up table.
PV2 evidence leaves hundreds pending; M01 checks only a component of outcome 001.
No single total score promotes a route or course.
