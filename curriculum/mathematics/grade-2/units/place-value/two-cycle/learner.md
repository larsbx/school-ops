# Groups keep the amount — learner book

Draft • M01 • L-MATH-001-01-01 • tens and ones, 0–99

Use counters, groups of ten, paper and a pencil. Directions may be read aloud.
You may speak, draw, point to objects, or ask an adult to write your explanation.
A partner may be another learner or your teacher.

## Our question

How can the groups change while the amount stays the same?

Say or show: one counter is our one-unit; one group of ten contains ten counters.
Use the words one, ten, group, amount and equal.

## Cycle A — Build and describe

Watch your teacher group fourteen counters. Name the grouped units. Explain
what happened to the counters when a group was made.

### A1 — Make groups

Start with sixteen counters. Make as many complete groups of ten as possible.
Show the tens and the extra ones. Explain how you know no counters disappeared.
