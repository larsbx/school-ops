# M01 — Partner card A

Draft • Practice only • Give this card to partner A alone.

## Cycle A: your clue

The red box contains two complete tens and four extra ones.

Describe its groups. Your partner needs to reconstruct them.
Ask your partner for the blue-box clue and build that collection.

## Cycle B: your clue

The green box contains one ten and sixteen ones.

Describe its groups without showing this card.
Ask your partner to pack complete tens and explain the unchanged amount.
Ask your partner for the gold-box clue and reconstruct that collection.
