This is a bounded response to the [Grade 3 arithmetic planning scaffold](../../scaffolds/grade-3/mathematics.md) and the [K–12 prerequisite mapping issue](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/issues/11), **not** a replacement for the existing [Grade 2 mathematics course](../grade-2/README.md). ## Bounded aim, selected arts and outcomes

Arithmetic is the primary Quadrivium art; geometry supplies equal-square array and partition representations, not a claim to complete a separate geometry course. Grammar names units, factors, equal shares, numerators and denominators; logic checks preservation and counters false claims; rhetoric explains a labeled representation so another learner can check it. **Music is excluded.** All lesson material and fixed tasks were originally authored for this prototype; no external exercise bank or licensed text is copied.

## Proposed teaching sequence (three distinct draft episodes)

These **three 25-minute examples** are proposed, not an agreed timetable or required unit sequence. A facilitator may use speech, drawing, movable paper squares, braille/tactile equivalents or assistive communication without supplying answers. Writing speed, device access and English fluency are not preconditions for demonstrating the mathematical idea.

## Proposed progression and nonpromotion conditions

- An entry failure on E1 routes to concrete 3-digit/zero-place work; E2–E3 route to movable grouping/sharing; E4 to equal-area tiles. Retest with different numbers before progressing. No automatic claim of mastery from finishing a page.
- For P1, require naming the **first differing place** as well as comparison. For P2, a correct total without a row/column explanation does not establish a split proof. For P3, counting shaded cells without checking equal-sized parts of the *same whole* does not establish fractional reasoning.
- Avoid an overall average masking a missing component. The teacher key specifies separate proposed thresholds and each task's expected reasoning; these are **review candidates**.
- Learner-facing sheets contain no key links. Teacher guides are original materials, **not an external resource-registration claim**.
- To register a course, the owner must agree subject boundary, formal identifiers, prerequisites, access/material options, time/calendar, numerical ranges, translation/language supports, child records and an independently administered assessment design under the [curriculum model](../../../docs/curriculum-model.md). Review the mathematics, diagrams and separate response-mode equivalence with a teacher. No teaching-ready or observed-learner claims are made.

This pilot contributes mathematics planning only. Across the 13×8 matrix, Islamic studies retains its separate [source, translation, hadith and fiqh admission gates](../../scaffolds/islamic-studies-guide.md); no religious claims or judgments appear here.
