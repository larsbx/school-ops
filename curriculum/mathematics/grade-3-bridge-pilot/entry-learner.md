# Grade 3 arithmetic bridge — learner entry check (draft; no answers)

**Use:** E1–E4 test prior relationships, not a grade placement label. Not a timed test; proposed 10–15 minutes only as an administration estimate. Paper squares/counters, large print, tactile markers, oral/scribed responses and accessible diagrams are permitted. A reader may state instructions but must not suggest the numbers or classifications. Original tasks; no student information belongs in Git.

**E1 — place-value preservation.** Show **470** using hundreds, tens and ones. Write or explain the expanded quantity. What does the final zero tell us? If you cannot draw bundles, describe them verbally.

**E2 — equal groups.** There are **3 trays with 5 counters on each**. Show, describe or draw the groups, give the total and say why 3 + 5 does not give the total.

**E3 — sharing.** Divide **12 counters equally among 4 boxes**. How many counters per box? State why none remain. A verbal allocation with equal-group sizes is valid.

**E4 — equal partitions.** Draw, arrange or describe an **8-cell rectangle** with **four equally sized parts**. How many cells in each part? Explain why making parts with 1, 2, 2 and 3 cells would not satisfy the instruction.

Do not include religious examples, private household details or individual responses in public repository changes. The adult retains a private teacher key separately. This learner sheet provides neither model answers nor a link to that key.
