# Teacher sequence — draft

Use accessible cards from [learner booklet](learner.md) and [teacher key](teacher-key.md). Large print, line breaks, movable tactile labels or keyboard navigation; no color-only distinctions. Read neutrally, allow speech, pointing, writing, diagrams or AAC. Never role-play sacred figures or ask about worship. Check E1/E2 and E4 access before episode 1; E3 before episode 2; remediate each missed skill. No prior enrollment or Arabic fluency assumed.

## Episode 1 — What was accepted? (P1)

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- |
| Grammar: read A/B and name proposal, accepted agreement, condition; align text strips. | 6 |
| Logic: G1/G2, compare changed wording; reject using A as controlling record. | 8 |
| Rhetoric: teach partner correction with B and day; partner requests evidence. | 7 |
| Independent X1; give feedback afterward. | 4 |

A later date alone does not establish acceptance. Ask which record states both parties accepted.

## Episode 2 — Condition is not prediction (P2)

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | --- |

| Rhetoric: explain unknown case and request evidence; partner checks relevance. | 6 |

Contrast absent return slip with a slip explicitly recording no return.

## Administration and real reading

The [source ledger](source-review.md) names a candidate actual report and conditional reading prompt/key. No real religious reading until qualified review. S1–S8 are exposed rehearsal only; suggest 20–25 minutes plus breaks, no speed criterion. Blank frames, neutral glossary, read-aloud and AAC retained; helper may transcribe, not select evidence. Fresh reviewed parallel forms required for independent use. Record only actual evidence privately; no adopted timetable or mastery inferred.
