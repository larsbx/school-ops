## Human review gates (all unresolved)

- [ ] Curriculum owner agrees Grade 3 course boundary, prerequisite thresholds, languages, resources and timetable; no inferred Grade 2 mastery.
- [ ] Qualified Islamic studies reviewer approves source locators, translations/tafsīr distinctions, hadith metadata and age appropriateness; selected approved source editions are recorded.
- [ ] Teacher validates learner tasks, keys, accessible response modes and independent administration; confirms P1/P2 thresholds and remediation.
- [ ] Legal/source rights and accessibility checks for any *future copied* text.
- [ ] Decision on authorized private learner evidence custody; keep student names, accommodations and scores out of public Git.
- [ ] Only after those gates may canonical IDs, a registered course and formal readiness review be proposed separately.
