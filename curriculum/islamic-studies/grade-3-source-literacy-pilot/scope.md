# Grade 3 Islamic studies source-literacy pilot — scope brief

- **Record kind/status:** draft scope, **not approved/ready**
- **Course IDs/outcome IDs:** unassigned; G3-IS-P1/P2 are local prototype labels
- **Learners:** Grade 3, starting knowledge to be assessed rather than presumed
- **Time/date:** authoring estimate of 2 × 25 minutes and 15–20-minute assessment; calendar and budget not agreed
- **Materials:** original text-free source metadata cards, read-aloud, print, pen, AAC/drawing as needed; authorized primary texts not selected
- **Arts:** grammar (accurate metadata/terminology), logic (source versus inference), rhetoric (well-attributed explanation). **No musical work.**

## Prerequisite evidence and remediation

- **E1:** classify 3/4 source-type cards from the [neutral entry check](draft-assessments/entry-check.md), without calling any invented example scripture. Reteach source labels using another neutral example if not demonstrated.
- **E3:** distinguish recorded observation from unsupported inference in **both** of two neutral pairs. Reteach `record says / I infer` if needed.
- **E4:** establish a usable response route in speech, drawing, writing, pointing or AAC. Language and disability access are **not learner failures**.
- Grade 2 unit attendance or marks are never presumed; E2 chronology will be checked only before separately authored history work.

## Scope boundaries

- Only source-type/classification reasoning and limits of inference for Grade 3 P1 and P2 are authored.
- The Qur'an reference 49:13 and hadith *Sahih al-Bukhari* 8 are **metadata locators**, not reproduced passages or an independent chain verification. Exact translation/edition is not chosen; child suitability, permissions and qualified scholarly review remain open.
- No sīrah chronology, ritual demonstration, legal-school ruling, recitation assessment, or real-world practice report is requested.
- This proposal requires curriculum-owner review, qualified religious-source review, material/access approval and source corpus selection before canonical registration and teaching.
