## Complete model answers

**Task 1:** A = Qur'an **locator**, not text; B = **translation metadata only** without wording or translator; C = **teacher explanation** (not religious tafsīr); D = hadith **report locator**, not an authenticated chain; E = **fictional classroom note**. A reasonable explanation points to a field (e.g., B says "translator: not yet selected").

**Task 2:** A = Qur'an sūrah **49**, āyah **13**; D = **Sahih al-Bukhari, report 8** (Book 2 is optional). **B** is the explicit no-translator/no-English-wording metadata record. A and D also have no copied text; do not infer source wording from them.

**Task 3:** T1 = verbatim words only from an **invented diary**; T2 = **teacher explanation**; T3 = **qualified student inference**; T4 = **unsupported historical/authenticity claim**. Nothing in D reports a Hijrah date or route, nor proves its chain's reliability.

**Task 4:** Example: "D gives a report collection and number; it does not show the Prophet's migration date or an independent authenticity study; B names no translator, edition or English words, so I cannot quote one." Equivalent evidence-backed answers accepted.

**Task 5 (formative):** e.g., "B says no translator or words are selected, so its metadata is not itself a translation; the exact English wording remains unknown." Do **not** compensate for wrong T4 attribution with eloquence.

## Outcome-specific criteria and remediation

| Local outcome | Independent evidence | Proposed criterion | Follow-up for missing evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **G3-IS-P1** | Task 1's five labels, Task 2's two locators | **At least 4/5** labels correct **and both** source locators correct | Reteach distinctions with *new neutral* cards, then recheck without showing key |
| **G3-IS-P2** | Task 3's four classifications, Task 4's evidence limit | **At least 3/4** correct **and** T4 labeled unsupported **and** Card B identified as lacking translator words | Model source vs inference using another neutral example, then reassess |

Any false claim that C/E is Qur'an, T1 is hadith, or D proves a hadith chain/Islamic history requires direct corrective feedback **even if a count threshold is met**. **No aggregate score, performance of worship, personal faith or family practice** establishes either outcome. These proposed rubrics require child-suitability and scholarly review before use.

A saved session must preserve its exact learner sheet and separated teacher key. Novel practice sessions must regenerate independent forms; this hardcoded exemplar is **not** the generator.
