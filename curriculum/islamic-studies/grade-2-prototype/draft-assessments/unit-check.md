# Draft end-of-unit assessment — Grade 2 sources, pillars and character

- Status: **draft assessment prototype; formal ID unassigned**
- Purpose: check local outcomes **IS2-P01–IS2-P04**, not religious devotion
- Proposed conditions: **20 minutes**, individual, source cards available with labels; teacher may read all questions and source cards aloud, but may not give answers
- Materials: [source cards and matching strip](../units/sources-pillars-character/teacher-pack.md), paper, pencil
- Authorization/review: pending; **not classroom-ready**

## Learner tasks

**Task 1 — Classify (P01).** Label each original source card: Q, H and S. For each, choose from **Qur'an passage reference / hadith report reference / fictional teacher scenario**. One mark per correct classification.

**Task 2 — Locate (P01).** A friend wants to look up Card Q and Card H. Write or tell the friend the chapter and verse for Q and the collection and report number for H. Do not copy any scripture words.

**Task 3 — Match (P02).** Pair each of these terms with one description: *shahādah, ṣalāh, zakāh, ṣawm of Ramadan, ḥajj*. Descriptions: (A) pilgrimage to Makkah; (B) testimony of faith; (C) obligatory charitable giving; (D) fasting in Ramadan; (E) prescribed prayer. Then name the report collection and number from which the teacher's list is adapted.

**Task 4 — Explain Card Q (P03).** State one idea from the teacher summary of Qur'an 49:13 about different peoples or groups. Answer: "Are the sentences on Card Q an exact quotation of the Qur'an, or a teacher's summary?" Explain your evidence.

**Task 5 — Reason about Card S (P04).** Sam cannot find a pencil; a classmate has a spare. Give one respectful action and a reason using the situation. State explicitly whether the scenario is (a) a Qur'anic quote, (b) a hadith quotation, or (c) an invented scenario. If you connect your action with a religious value, say whether this is your **application/inference** rather than direct words of Card Q or H.

## Rubric — each local outcome independently

| Outcome | Evidence to inspect | Threshold for demonstrated understanding | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IS2-P01 | Task 1 labels and Task 2 locators | **All three labels correct and both locators sufficiently identifiable** (Q: 49:13, H: Sahih al-Bukhari report 8) | Repeat comparison with new clearly attributed examples |
| IS2-P02 | Task 3 pairs and source | **At least 4/5 pairs correct and H08 identified as report source** | Reteach mislabeled terms and repeat independent matching |
| IS2-P03 | Task 4 main idea and evidence boundary | **Mentions people/groups getting to know each other and explicitly calls card a teacher summary, not Qur'anic quote** | Revisit selected approved source versus card text; repeat explanation |
| IS2-P04 | Task 5 action, reason and attribution | **Feasible respectful action, reason grounded in Sam's problem, and unambiguous statement that Card S is invented and conclusion is an inference** | Model distinctions, then assess a parallel fictional case |

**No averaged score is sufficient:** identify outcomes with missing evidence separately. Accept oral, pointing or drawn answers with teacher transcription; supports must not supply requested answers. A response about personal belief or ritual performance earns no score.

## Teacher answer key and model evidence

- **Task 1:** Q → Qur'anic passage **reference**; H → hadith report **reference**; S → teacher-invented scenario. Neither Q nor H contains a direct quoted source translation.
- **Task 2:** Q → sūrah 49, āyah 13; H → *Sahih al-Bukhari*, report 8, in-book Book 2, Hadith 1.
- **Task 3:** shahādah → B; ṣalāh → E; zakāh → C; ṣawm of Ramadan → D; ḥajj → A. Attribution: teacher summary of *Sahih al-Bukhari* report 8; names and descriptions are not claimed to reproduce its English text.
- **Task 4:** An acceptable response: "The summary says people are different groups and are to get to know one another. Card Q tells me this is the teacher's summary; it is not an exact Qur'an quotation." Do not accept an unsupported claim that Card Q's prose is a translated verse.
- **Task 5:** An acceptable response: "I could offer my spare pencil because Sam needs one. Card S is a fictional situation made by a teacher; helping is my conclusion about that situation, not a quote from a hadith or verse." Other respectful, feasible answers with reasons qualify.
