# Draft entry check — Grade 2 Islamic studies source literacy

- Status: **draft assessment prototype; ID unassigned**
- Purpose: determine starting comprehension and source-label familiarity, not faith or worship observance
- Intended grade: 2
- Conditions: **8–10 minutes** proposed, individual oral or drawn answer accepted; teacher reads cards without coaching; pencil/paper optional
- Materials: [teacher pack](../units/sources-pillars-character/teacher-pack.md)
- Review: pending

## Tasks (actual prompts)

1. Show Card S. Ask: "Tell what happened first and what happened next." Record sequence coherence.
2. Show the *source label only* of Card Q. Ask: "Does this label name a Qur'anic passage, a hadith collection, or a made-up classroom situation? What number would help you find it?"
3. Show the *source label only* of Card H. Ask the same question and request the report number.
4. Ask: "Can a teacher's summary of a text be the exact same thing as the text? Explain why or why not." Accept "I do not know" as entry evidence.

## Entry evidence table and key

| Entry skill | Expected model evidence | Interpret and respond |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sequence two events | Sam needed a pencil; another classmate offered one; returning was requested | If order is unclear, demonstrate a short retell before Lesson 1 |
| Identify Qur'an locator | Card Q: Qur'an 49:13, chapter/verse | If unknown, begin with labeled card sorting |
| Identify hadith locator | Card H: Sahih al-Bukhari, report 8 | If unknown, begin with report-label comparison |
| Distinguish summary from source text | Teacher's words describe what source may discuss but are not the cited original | If unknown, explicitly model the distinction |

This is **diagnostic, not a pass/fail threshold**. Missing skills prompt instruction, not grade advancement or religious evaluation. Do not store learner details in Git.
