# Scope brief — Grade 2 Islamic studies prototype

- Status: **draft scope proposal; not accepted**
- Formal course ID: **unassigned**
- Intended learners: second-grade pupils, with no assumed Qur'an literacy, recitation skill or worship history
- Framework: grammar (terms and citations), logic (source versus inference), rhetoric (respectful, checkable explanation)
- Review date / supporting PR: pending

## Purpose and starting point

Learners will identify named textual sources without confusing a teacher's paraphrase with the Qur'an or a hadith text, identify the five pillars at an introductory level, and give reasons for respectful actions in classroom scenarios. Check starting comprehension before setting pace: [entry check](draft-assessments/entry-check.md).

| Item | Authoring proposal / unresolved decision |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | Oral retelling and source-label recognition, measured by entry check |
| Outcomes | The four **local** outcome labels in [prototype course](README.md); no canonical IDs |
| Teaching time | Proposed 4 × 25 minutes and 1 × 20-minute check; not agreed |
| Calendar | Not selected |
| Materials | Teacher-authored source-card summaries, actual external references, pencils and paper; use read-aloud and oral-response alternatives |
| Primary religious studies | Qur'an citation, labeled hadith report, five pillars, akhlāq |
| Text choices | Qur'an 49:13 and Sahih al-Bukhari report 8 as **external references**; qualified review and authorized translation/access required before use |
| Sīrah, memorization, recitation and practical worship | Excluded from this first **authored** unit; potential later scope |
| Boundaries | No doctrinal grading, compulsory worship evidence, private student records or unaudited authenticity claims |

## Open decisions

Teaching tradition / community review; approved source texts and translations; language and accessibility; classroom duration; teacher expertise; child-suitability review; source rights and access; any additional sīrah or hadith studies.

See the [K–12 Islamic studies subject guide](../../scaffolds/islamic-studies-guide.md). These decisions block a `ready` designation but not the drafting of original teaching materials.
