## Observable goal

**IS2-P01:** Three of three labels and accurate mention of surah/ayah for Q and collection/report number for H. See [unit check](../../../draft-assessments/unit-check.md) for the independent end-of-unit criterion.

## Trivium work

- **Grammar:** use Qur'an, surah/ayah, hadith/report, or ethical terms from the card with correct meaning and attribution.
- **Logic:** identify what evidence the named card actually provides; distinguish inference from source wording and test one incorrect claim.
- **Rhetoric:** give an ordered explanation aloud, in writing or with a labeled diagram and oral narration.

## Activities

| Stage | Original teacher prompt and action | Minutes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Entry and retrieval | Read aloud Cards Q, H and S. Ask: What would let us find Q or H again? Which is an imaginary example? | 4 |
| Guided source encounter | Present the three cards without headers (retain citation tags and summaries). Learners mark each as Qur'an citation, hadith collection report, or original scenario and underline the locator. | 7 |
| Supported practice | With a partner, explain why a teacher summary is not automatically the cited original text. | 7 |
| Independent practice | Write or draw an additional original example and label which card it came from or why it is a personal inference. | 4 |
| Independent exit check | Exit: label Q/H/S correctly and name one lookup locator for each religious source. | 3 |
| **Total** | | **25** |

## Teacher key, feedback and progression

**Evidence threshold:** Three of three labels and accurate mention of surah/ayah for Q and collection/report number for H.

**Expected reasoning:** Refer to the [card-specific model answers](../teacher-pack.md). Accept faithful oral/drawn explanations, not unsupported text copying or unreviewed authenticity assertions.

**Next action if absent:** Reteach one mislabeled card and ask the learner to classify a newly labeled card. Do not substitute memorized devotion for source recognition.

The next lesson is a proposed progression, not confirmation of demonstrated mastery. Individual learner evidence stays private. Source wording, accessibility and qualified review still block teaching readiness.
