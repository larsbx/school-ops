## Observable goal

**IS2-P02:** At least four of five correct matches on the individual check, and cite Card H as a report from Sahih al-Bukhari 8. See [unit check](../../../draft-assessments/unit-check.md) for the independent end-of-unit criterion.

## Trivium work

- **Grammar:** use Qur'an, surah/ayah, hadith/report, or ethical terms from the card with correct meaning and attribution.
- **Logic:** identify what evidence the named card actually provides; distinguish inference from source wording and test one incorrect claim.
- **Rhetoric:** give an ordered explanation aloud, in writing or with a labeled diagram and oral narration.

## Activities

| Stage | Original teacher prompt and action | Minutes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Entry and retrieval | Recall which source card names a hadith report and its lookup number. | 4 |
| Guided source encounter | Read Card H's original teacher summary and the matching strip; distinguish teaching labels from a direct hadith quotation. | 7 |
| Supported practice | Match the shuffled five terms to five descriptions; discuss why sharing a spare pencil is not, by itself, formal zakāh. | 7 |
| Independent practice | Write or draw an additional original example and label which card it came from or why it is a personal inference. | 4 |
| Independent exit check | Each learner gives two pillar-description matches and explains where the teacher list came from. | 3 |
| **Total** | | **25** |

## Teacher key, feedback and progression

**Evidence threshold:** At least four of five correct matches on the individual check, and cite Card H as a report from Sahih al-Bukhari 8.

**Expected reasoning:** Refer to the [card-specific model answers](../teacher-pack.md). Accept faithful oral/drawn explanations, not unsupported text copying or unreviewed authenticity assertions.

**Next action if absent:** If a term is mixed up, use an example/nonexample and retry a fresh match. No pupil is required to perform any act of worship.

The next lesson is a proposed progression, not confirmation of demonstrated mastery. Individual learner evidence stays private. Source wording, accessibility and qualified review still block teaching readiness.
