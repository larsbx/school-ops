## Observable goal

**IS2-P04:** A feasible respectful action, a reason tied to scenario details, and an accurate source-versus-inference label. See [unit check](../../../draft-assessments/unit-check.md) for the independent end-of-unit criterion.

## Trivium work

- **Grammar:** use Qur'an, surah/ayah, hadith/report, or ethical terms from the card with correct meaning and attribution.
- **Logic:** identify what evidence the named card actually provides; distinguish inference from source wording and test one incorrect claim.
- **Rhetoric:** give an ordered explanation aloud, in writing or with a labeled diagram and oral narration.

## Activities

| Stage | Original teacher prompt and action | Minutes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Entry and retrieval | Review one difference among Card Q, Card H and Card S. | 4 |
| Guided source encounter | Read Card S. Ask for possible respectful responses; distinguish facts of the scenario from opinions or ethical advice. | 7 |
| Supported practice | Learners prepare a three-part explanation: (1) action, (2) reason from the scenario, (3) exact card or 'my reasoning' label. Partners test whether the cited card really says that. | 7 |
| Independent practice | Write or draw an additional original example and label which card it came from or why it is a personal inference. | 4 |
| Independent exit check | Exit: explain an action and give a checkable reason without attributing invented classroom dialogue to scripture or hadith. | 3 |
| **Total** | | **25** |

## Teacher key, feedback and progression

**Evidence threshold:** A feasible respectful action, a reason tied to scenario details, and an accurate source-versus-inference label.

**Expected reasoning:** Refer to the [card-specific model answers](../teacher-pack.md). Accept faithful oral/drawn explanations, not unsupported text copying or unreviewed authenticity assertions.

**Next action if absent:** If the learner falsely attributes a command or story to scripture/hadith, return to labels before a new scenario. Do not assess the child's own private behavior.

The next lesson is a proposed progression, not confirmation of demonstrated mastery. Individual learner evidence stays private. Source wording, accessibility and qualified review still block teaching readiness.
