## Observable goal

**IS2-P03:** Correct source locator + theme about people knowing one another + accurate summary-versus-quotation distinction. See [unit check](../../../draft-assessments/unit-check.md) for the independent end-of-unit criterion.

## Trivium work

- **Grammar:** use Qur'an, surah/ayah, hadith/report, or ethical terms from the card with correct meaning and attribution.
- **Logic:** identify what evidence the named card actually provides; distinguish inference from source wording and test one incorrect claim.
- **Rhetoric:** give an ordered explanation aloud, in writing or with a labeled diagram and oral narration.

## Activities

| Stage | Original teacher prompt and action | Minutes |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Entry and retrieval | Ask learners to find the chapter and verse locator on Card Q. | 4 |
| Guided source encounter | Read Card Q's teacher summary, then present two statements: (A) 'Card Q mentions different peoples getting to know each other' and (B) 'Card Q contains a word-for-word English Qur'anic quotation.' Ask which is supported. | 7 |
| Supported practice | Learners explain why a summary can support a topic discussion but cannot substitute for an approved Qur'anic translation. | 7 |
| Independent practice | Write or draw an additional original example and label which card it came from or why it is a personal inference. | 4 |
| Independent exit check | Exit: name 49:13 as Qur'anic source and explain one theme of the card, noting that its words are teacher-authored. | 3 |
| **Total** | | **25** |

## Teacher key, feedback and progression

**Evidence threshold:** Correct source locator + theme about people knowing one another + accurate summary-versus-quotation distinction.

**Expected reasoning:** Refer to the [card-specific model answers](../teacher-pack.md). Accept faithful oral/drawn explanations, not unsupported text copying or unreviewed authenticity assertions.

**Next action if absent:** If an unsupported direct quotation is claimed, compare source card headers and direct learner to the selected reviewed translation when available.

The next lesson is a proposed progression, not confirmation of demonstrated mastery. Individual learner evidence stays private. Source wording, accessibility and qualified review still block teaching readiness.
