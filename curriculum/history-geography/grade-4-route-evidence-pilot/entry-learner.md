# Learner entry checks E1–E4 (draft)

**Untimed fictional prompts, not a secure assessment.** Oral, written, tactile, pointing or AAC responses are allowed. Teacher should provide accessible instructions but no correct-answer cues during an independent check.

**E1.** Toy map legend: Oak–Pine is 2 km, Pine–Reed is 4 km. What does “Oak–Pine 2 km” mean? Does that tell you the number of minutes?

**E2.** A connected route has one 3 km segment and one 2 km segment. Write or say the total distance, sum and unit.

**E3.** A made-up library notice states “The desk opens at ten.” A visitor says “The room will be crowded at ten.” Which is the notice's statement, and which is an unsupported forecast?

**E4.** Invented event A is dated Year 1 day 12; event B is dated Year 2 day 03. Which happened first according to the stated records? Does the ordering establish why B happened?

Rechecks after teaching must use distinct material; none of these examples proves learner mastery.
