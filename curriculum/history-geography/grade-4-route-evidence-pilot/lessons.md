# Four proposed 25-minute teaching episodes

**Draft instructional authoring, NOT an adopted schedule.** Fictional sources from [the original packet](source-packet.md) only. Each episode has grammar, logic/dialectic and rhetoric; numerical work adds genuine arithmetic and connected-map geometry. Use supported response modes throughout. X1–X4 are uncoached formative **exemplars**, keyed separately in [teacher guide](guided-teacher.md). Phases each total **25 minutes**.

## Episode 1 — map legend, edition and connected length (P1; E1/E2)
| Minutes | Trivium and observable work |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | **Grammar:** name endpoint, segment, legend, kilometre and edition; read M1's Alder–Birch and Birch–Cedar entries. |
| 6 | **Logic:** does M1 prove Year 2 availability or travel minutes? Give a counterexample: no Year 2 status or speed stated. |
| 7 | **Arithmetic/geometry:** trace connected Year 1 Alder → Birch → Cedar and form **4 + 3**; contrast a pair of disconnected edges. |
| 4 | **Rhetoric:** explain the edition, endpoints, route sum and missing travel-time information to an absent reader. |
| 3 | **Independent X1:** In M1, name Alder–Dune's length; does it give travel minutes? |

**Feedback:** Confusing km and minutes calls for a reworked legend example and a fresh route, not silently correcting the answer.

## Episode 2 — changed road status and alternate paths (P2; E1/E2)
| Minutes | Trivium and observable work |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | **Grammar:** distinguish open/closed, edition, alternative and shorter **by distance**. |
| 6 | **Logic:** challenge “M1's 7-km Alder–Birch–Cedar route is usable in Year 2”; identify the closed edge via M2 and N1. |
| 7 | **Arithmetic/geometry:** trace Alder–Dune–Cedar and Alder–Birch–Dune–Cedar; add 5+4 and 4+2+4. |
| 4 | **Rhetoric:** compare reachable distances; explain why shorter **does not prove faster** with no speeds supplied. |
| 3 | **Independent X2:** How long is Year 2 Alder–Birch–Dune–Cedar, and is it shorter than Alder–Dune–Cedar? |

**Feedback:** An arithmetic shortcut through a closed edge is ineligible. Recheck reachability separately from addition.

## Episode 3 — date, provenance, report and unsupported generalization (P3; E3/E4)
| Minutes | Trivium and observable work |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | **Grammar:** distinguish map, notice N1, plan P1, diary D1, inspection log L1 and their dates. |
| 6 | **Logic:** distinguish N1's closure from P1's **proposal**, D1's one observation from “all travelers,” and L1's inspection from safety approval. |
| 7 | **Chronology:** order N1 day 06-01, P1 day 06-02, D1 day 06-03, L1 day 06-04; distinguish recorded order from cause. |
| 4 | **Rhetoric:** give a checkable “record says / can conclude / still unknown” argument. |
| 3 | **Independent X3:** Does D1 establish all travelers met mud on every day? Give the limit. |

**Feedback:** Require specific words from the document type and explain why a generalization exceeds its scope.

## Episode 4 — justified route memo (P4; E1–E4)
| Minutes | Trivium and observable work |
| ---: | --- |
| 5 | **Grammar:** recall dated map, length, notice, evidence, inference and caveat. |
| 6 | **Logic:** rebut “use the old 7-km closed route because shorter always means fastest.” Identify closure and missing time evidence. |
| 7 | **Arithmetic/geometry:** justify Year 2 Alder–Dune–Cedar with **5 + 4 = 9 km** against another eligible path. |
| 4 | **Rhetoric:** construct recommendation, two attributed reasons (M2 and N1), counterpoint and explicit uncertainty; invite reasoned critique. |
| 3 | **Independent X4:** Deliver a two-sentence Year 2 memo with viable route and sum, M2+N1, and one unsupported inference to avoid. |

**Feedback:** Give targeted feedback separately for route math, source support and caveat; any recheck uses an equivalent *new* scenario, not the published answer. No family travel accounts or real student data are collected.
