# Guided learner tasks G1–G8

**Public guided practice, draft and not independent mastery evidence.** Consult the [invented packet](source-packet.md). Respond by route tracing, accessible table, speech or writing with units and reasons.

**G1.** In M1, identify Alder–Birch's endpoints and distance. Does the map give time in minutes?

**G2.** In M1, compute Alder → Birch → Dune as two connected lengths.

**G3.** In M1 Year 1, compute Alder → Birch → Cedar.

**G4.** A pupil recommends G3 in Year 2 because seven is small. Which edge makes this ineligible under M2 and N1?

**G5.** In Year 2 compare Alder → Dune → Cedar and Alder → Birch → Dune → Cedar: sums, shorter distance, difference and whether faster is established.

**G6.** What precisely does N1 state about start date? Does it prove the segment never reopens?

**G7.** Can P1 establish a completed delivery? What does D1 report, and what does it not establish?

**G8.** Give a Year 2 Alder-to-Cedar route recommendation citing M2 and N1, computing a kilometre sum and stating one source limit.
