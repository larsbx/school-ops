# Northwick paper district — original **fictional** source packet

Every place, date, distance, map, notice, planner and diary below is invented for practice, **not actual history or travel advice**. Each map edge is a road segment traversable both directions for this *paper exercise* unless marked closed. Only listed connections exist; **no direct Alder–Cedar segment**. Kilometres (km) measure listed route length, NOT minutes, speed, risk or straight-line distance. Labels M1/M2/N1/P1/D1/L1 are temporary source references, not approved resource IDs.
